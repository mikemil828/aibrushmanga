# «Кисть» — what the engine can do and how to work with it

An oil painting engine: the picture comes from the brush hairs, the paint on them, the pressure and the relief of the paint lying there; there are no ready-made textures or stamps. This file is where to start: the working cycle, an example on a clean canvas, the main things about the brush, the unexpected in the engine. It says what the tool does, not how to paint a picture. Fields, brushes and technique recipes — `СПРАВОЧНИК.md` (search: `python -m кисть.справка <слово>`). How the engine is built — the working `README.md`, no need to read it.

## 1. Working cycle

Paint **with a Python program**, piece by piece: laid, looked, measured, rewrote. A piece is what is written by one function and checked by one crop.

1. **Load and a probe.** Much of what a sweep will give is known as a number before laying, without trials or drying (the map, row «Know before laying»): load for a coverage over dry — `н = нагрузка_для(кисть, покрытие=0.9, нажим=0.6)` (for non-opaque paint — with the color: `нагрузка_для(кисть, покрытие=0.3, цвет=Краска((240, 235, 225), 'прозрачная'))`, without it — an error), what your own load will give — `по_таблице(кисть, 0.3)`; a series of overlapping rows — `план_покрытия(кисть, 0.6, шаг_рядов=0.7)`; furrows of a sweep — `борозды_для(кисть, нагрузка=0.5, цвет=(70, 80, 110))` or to order `σ=5`; a sweep over wet paint lying there — `смешение_для(кисть, место, холст=х, цвет=…, нагрузка=0.3, слой=…)` (`слой=` and `мешать_ниже=` — the same as for the stroke) or to order `доля=0.3`; softness of the edge — `край_для(кисть, кайма_мм=0.3, по_сырому=True)`; how much lightness the core of the white paint will reach — `предел_светлоты(кисть, (255, 255, 251), подложка=(250, 238, 205), нагрузка=0.5)` (16 fields of its own test bench with this brush, 4–16 s with one pass, two or three passes with a brush of 10 mm and wider — up to 37 s: `'L'` and `'σ'` — by how much one field will drift), where its ceiling is — with `предел=True`, load and passes to order — with `L=`. The unattainable is an error with the nearest attainable; one sweep wanders around the number by the answer's `'разброс'`. Scattered sweeps, sweeps along your own path and with `запас`, with a set of paints are not known to the tables — there check the brush and the load on a small canvas or with `о, картинка = х.проба(…)` (`о` — the stroke response, see step 2): does it cover the ground, what texture, what tone. Several values at once — `п = х.перебор(мазки, нагрузка=[0.006, 0.012, 0.025], путь='перебор.png')`: a batch of strokes at each value in processes, a sheet «before | variants» and the measurements of each (`print(п)`), the canvas is untouched; the one that fits — `х.взять(п[k])` (handbook, «Gaps and scan»). The handbook's numbers are working ones, not exact.
2. **Laying.** A piece is laid by a loop in Python: places, color, brush, path. Color — by a palette mix by name or your own for each sweep: a palette is a decision that is visible in the journal by name, and editing a mix rebuilds all its strokes; a color for each sweep taken from the reference is the signature of a render (the map, «Lay: paint»). Before the piece — `снимок = х.снимок()` and `х.пометка('…', этап=…, объект=…)`. **The stroke answers with a measurement:** `о = х.мазок(…)` — the rectangle of the trace and what came out: `о.цвет` (tone of the trace on the canvas, L, C, h), `о.закрыто` (how much the paint covered what lay there), `о.просветы` (share of the trace without paint), `о.ширина`, `о.сырое` (how much wet it picked up), `о.разрывы` (where the sweep broke and why), `о.край` (edge band px, hard, soft or ragged); `print(о)` — as a line. Read the response of the first strokes of a batch — a wrong color, gaps and a broken sweep are visible immediately, without a crop (handbook, «Stroke response»).
3. **Crop.** After the piece — `х.взгляд(область, масштаб, рядом=образец, путь='кроп.png')`: an 8-bit PNG, canvas and reference (picture, canvas, `.npz` or a color function f(x, y)) the same place at the same scale; look at it with your eyes (open the picture), not only by number. 1:1 or ×2–×4, up to ~600×600 px; do not open the whole sheet. The whole sheet or a whole large piece — `х.виды(область, путь='виды.png', цель=образец)`: in one PNG the squint (tone masses) next to the squint of the reference, tone, saturation, warm-cool, edges, thickness, wetness, directions and widths of strokes from the journal; `х.обзор(цель, путь=…)` — from afar and the worst places 1:1. A layer's contribution — `взгляд` and `виды` with `без=['слой']` or `до='слой'`. What stands where on the reference in canvas px — `х.сетка(образец, укладка='вписать', рядом=True, путь='сетка.png')`: a grid with labels over the canvas and the reference laid onto the canvas (of another proportion — `'вписать'`, `'обрезать'`, `'растянуть'`), `.укладка` — scale and shift reference → canvas. The whole sheet after a stage — as a draft at ¼ by the same journal: `python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --scale 0.25 --out черновик.png` — the same sheet four times smaller, four times faster than 1:1 (14 thousand strokes — ~1.5 min on one core); masses and tone — by it, texture and furrows — by a 1:1 crop; a soft and a narrow brush keep px in the draft and drift from the tuning — the replay's summary names their number, air and fine things — by 1:1.
4. **Measurement.** By number — with the stroke response and canvas calls, not your own code: `х.разность(цель, область)` (dL map and summary: mean, σ, worst windows), the gauges `х.окна`, `х.серая`, `х.темнее`, `х.борозды`, falloff across a joint `х.переход`, mean color `х.средний(область)`, where the ground or a lower layer is visible — `х.просвет(область, слой=…)` (a map and holes with place and area; `путь=` — «canvas | map»); target color — `цвет(L, C, h)`. Lightness everywhere is L OKLab in the gray scale 0–255 (handbook, «Eyes»). The number confirms the eye, it does not replace it. Next to the reference of a painting — `print(х.манера(область, образец='образец.png', px_на_мм_образца=…, объект=…))`: palette and mixes, tone within a patch, edges, directions, texture streak and stroke width of the canvas and the reference with one ruler and what in the canvas is narrower or wider, by how many times (handbook, «Manner from a reference»).
5. **Mirror after a series.** Your own zone fill (mask, direction field, color by formula, places by a grid with jitter) — `print(х.однообразие())` without selection: the worst places of the sheet (object × 50 mm cell) with a frame and what is narrow, `путь=` — a map over the canvas; the same line comes as the hint «рамка …» by itself. Finished a series of one technique (dozens of sweeps in one loop) — `print(х.однообразие(объект=…))` (or `слой=…`, `мазки=[номера строк журнала]`): what is the same in the series (lengths, slants, widths, pressure, brushes, colors) and places — grid or chance; places alone — `х.регулярность(объект=…)['слово']`; for `х.варианты` — `в.однообразие`. Monotony is not visible in a 1:1 crop, it is visible from afar on the whole sheet. «Узко» or «сетка» — fix the series loop (spread of lengths, slants, spacing), not all the painting work anew. One value is wrong (load, pressure) — `х.перебор(мазки, нагрузка=[…])` on the piece, not a rerun of the stage for the sake of a number.
6. **Correction.** Not right — decide yourself, looking at the crop, what to change, and **rewrite the piece** with another brush, load, order; do not patch on top. In a live Python session — `х.откатить(снимок)`: canvas and journal as before the piece. Several attempts of a piece at once — `вв = х.варианты(кусок, n, область)` (a piece is a function `кусок(участок, i)`, n attempts in processes), choose by eye from the crops `в.картинка` (linear light; into PNG — `из_линейного`) and `х.взять(вв[k])`: into the canvas and into the journal, as if the piece had been painted directly (handbook, «Variants of a piece»). Choosing between launches — `х.сохранить(…)`, then `х.варианты(…, в_папку='варианты/место')`: the script has ended, you look at `варианты/место/рядом.png`, the next script — `Холст.загрузить(…, журнал=…)` and `х.взять('варианты/место/2')`, bit for bit as the piece painted directly (handbook, «Variants on disk»). Saw later that what lies below is wrong (the bottom is cool, the wrong mix) — do not paint over it and do not edit the jsonl with your own code: `о = х.править(слой='низ', область=…, теплее=0.02, путь='до_после.png')` — edits the journal lines and rebuilds only what is above them and beside them (handbook, «Retroactive edits»). And so on, to the next piece.

One attempt is one run. Do not go through answers prepared in advance until you hit the needed number: that is fitting, not looking. What you saw and what you are changing — write down in the code next to the attempt and in the journal `х.заметка({…})`.

The canvas writes the journal itself: `Холст(…, журнал='лист.jsonl')`. Every stroke, removal, drying, wiping and charging of the brush is a line; a rollback removes the lines too; a trial does not go into the journal. Do not make up journal lines by hand: it is needed to rebuild the sheet, not to paint with it.

An attempt from the example (`примеры/цикл.py`; `пробник` — step 1, `писать_лист` — the piece, `кроп` and `замер` — steps 3 and 4; `ПОПЫТКИ` — lines «what I saw → what I change»). The example is imported without computing: `from примеры.цикл import пробник, писать_лист, кроп, замер`:

```python
НАГРУЗКА = пробник(ГРУНТ)
х = Холст(int(640 * м), int(400 * м), грунт=ГРУНТ, px_на_мм=8.0, журнал=os.path.join(куда, 'цикл.jsonl'))   # журнал пишется сам
п = ПОПЫТКИ[попытка - 1]
снимок = х.снимок()                                  # до куска: откат вернёт холст и журнал
о = писать_лист(х, м, НАГРУЗКА, п['разброс'], п['ровно'])     # ответы мазков: что вышло у каждого хода
print(о['сырое'][0])                                 # ответ мазка словами: цвет, закрыто, ширина, сырое, край
print(f'попытка {попытка} — кроп:', кроп(х, куда, f'цикл_{попытка}.png'), '· замер:', замер(х, о, м))
х.заметка({'попытка': попытка, 'увидел': п['увидел'], 'меняю': п['меняю'], 'замер': замер(х, о, м)}, этап=4, объект='край')
```

## Map: what is at hand

Everything the engine gives — by needs, not by pictures. In a row are all the tools for one need and how they differ; which to take, how many times, in what order and with what numbers — you decide: this is a crossroads, not a route, and for each need there are several roads. On the right is the handbook section and its lines (`sed -n a,bp СПРАВОЧНИК.md`); by word — `python -m кисть.справка <слово>`; the call's arguments — `help(х.взгляд)`.

| **Wet, dry, layers** | a layer is created by name in a stroke: paint mixes with the wet of its own layer, `мешать_ниже` — with the lower ones too · `х.подсушить`, `х.схватить` — fully dry or with a skin, everything, one layer or `область=` · dried paint stands under the brush: a sweep levels only the wet of its own layer, the relief of the dry — it does not · a flowing layer over dry (`разбавитель`, `ровно`; colorless — `МЕДИУМ`) flows into the hollows of what lies there: the ripple of fine painting closes, a thick sweep stands · `х.убрать_слой`, `х.порядок_слоёв` — retroactive edits of layers (go into the journal) · to see a layer's contribution — `без=`, `до=` of `взгляд`, `виды`, `обзор`, `просвет`, `манера`, and `до=` of `темнее` · to move the wet lying there — with a wiped brush, to remove — `х.снять(…, 'тряпка')` or `'скребок'` · to scratch out a light line over wet dark — `х.снять(точки, 'черенок', ширина=8)`: the wet is pushed apart into beads, from pressure 0.8 the lower layer is open · to correct a dried place: `х.прижать` — a knife sweep cuts off a dry bank and ridges, `х.размочить` — the dry is again taken by blending, rag and knife · `Холст(…, зерно=)` — canvas with a thread instead of a smooth ground | «Concepts», 38–48; «Drying, trial, rollback, removal, output», 283–349 |

## 2. Full example: a sheet of trials on a clean canvas

`примеры/цикл.py` lays on gray ground (150, 141, 126) a sheet of 640×400 px of four trials, each in its own layer, and measures them by the stroke responses (`писать_лист` returns them by trial) and by the gauge `х.борозды`; it has no pixel counting of its own:

```bash
cd движок && python3 примеры/цикл.py /tmp/цикл --попытка 1   # ~4 с на попытку; без --попытка — последняя; --мал — вдвое меньше
```

- **Probe:** a sweep of the flat 100 px with load 0.24 / 0.45 / 0.6 through `х.проба`: `о.просветы` 3.12 % / 0.43 % / 0.04 % (`о.закрыто` 0.81 / 0.95 / 0.98); the smallest with gaps under 0.5 % is taken — 0.45.
- **Fill:** flat 100 px, two overlapping rows, sweeps past the edges. Attempt 1 — the brush as is: furrows on the crop, the ground shows through along them in threads (`о.просветы` 0.49 %, `х.борозды` σ 12.6 L). Attempt 2 — `ровно=0.4`: the furrows are gone, gaps 0 %, σ 5.4 L.
- **Pressure scale:** `круглая` 32 px, pressure 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.8 — `о.ширина` 6 / 16 / 28 / 31 px. For `плоская` the width hardly depends on pressure (30–32 px).
- **Wet into wet:** a light sweep of the flat 24 px across a dark band (load 0.3, pressure 0.75). The response of a sweep over the wet of its own layer: `(133, 236, 186, 346) · цвет L 106 C 0.020 h 259 #636b76 · закрыто 0.95, просветы 0.00 · ширина 24 px · по сырому 0.93, подобрано 0.42 · ход по сырому чужому цвету: подобрано 1.52 к положенному, тон сдвинут на ΔL -114 / Δh +166°, Δa, b 0.047 (лежало L 60 #343c4e, названо L 220 #e2dcc8) · край жёсткий (кайма 1.0 px, рваность 1.2 px)` — it mixes with the dark (`о.цвет['L']` 106, `о.сырое['подобрано']` 0.42); over dried paint in a new layer — clean: L 208, picked up 0.
- **Edge of the soft brush:** `мягкая` 48 px, load 0.05; `о.край['кайма']` (10–50 % of the level of the trace body, on one side) at pressure 0.35 — 4 px (`'мягкий'`), at 0.85 — 2 px (`'жёсткий'`).
- **Journal:** `цикл.jsonl` alongside — the labels of the four trials and the attempt's note; `python -m кисть.повтор цикл.jsonl --out повтор.png` gives the same sheet bit for bit.

## 3. Connecting

Python 3, `numpy` and `scipy`; `Pillow` — to write crops and read pictures. The package `кисть` lies in `движок/`, the names in it are Russian.

`import sys; sys.path.insert(0, '<путь>/движок')`, then `from кисть import Холст, Кисть, Краска, жест, путь_с_нажимом, нажим_вдоль`.

The main things in one line each (in detail — the handbook):

- `х = Холст(ш, в, грунт=(r, g, b), px_на_мм=8.0, журнал='лист.jsonl')` — px, y down; the physics is tuned for 8 px/mm; the journal is written by itself.
- `х.пометка('заливка', этап=1, объект='заливка')` — the start of a stage or object: the fields `"stage"`, `"object"` on all lines after it; `х.заметка({…})` — what you saw, what you rejected, measurements; `намерение='…'` of a stroke — `"intent"`.
- `к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=100, разброс_тона=0.3)` — a preset and field replacements; the brush remembers dirt until `к.вытереть()`.
- `х.мазок(жест(опоры, ширина, 'остановка', сид=…, нажим=0.65), цвет, к, слой='проба', нагрузка=0.3)` — one sweep; `жест` builds a path with pressure from anchor points. The path's seed is each sweep's own: a stroke can be inserted or removed, the others will not shift (one `rng` per stage shifts all the following ones).
- `х.подсушить(1.0)` — fully dry; time does not pass by itself.
- `с = х.снимок()` … `х.откатить(с)` — bit for bit as it was; `х.проба(точки, цвет, к, …)` — one stroke for trial.
- `х.картинка(свет=True, сила=0.2, область=…)` — numpy, linear light; `х.png(путь)`, `х.сохранить('лист.npz')`.
- The same seed and the same brush history — the same pixels.
- Strokes in different places of the canvas are computed on the machine's cores by themselves (`Холст(…, ядра=N)`, without it — `KIST_CORES` or the machine's cores, up to 16; `ядра=1` — everything immediately, in this process): canvas, brushes and journal — byte for byte as on one core. A stroke finishes computing later than the call; reading the canvas, a layer or a brush (`картинка`, `х.слои[…]`, `проба`, `сохранить`, drying, `к.копия()`) finishes computing the strokes by itself.

## 4. The main things about the brush

Facts of the engine that decide the laying; numbers — the handbook, «Recipes».

- **A layer is what mixes.** A stroke mixes only with the wet paint of its own layer; over dried paint or in another layer it lies clean (example: L 106 against 208).
- **Time does not pass by itself.** Paint dries only from `х.подсушить(доля, область=…)`; without drying everything laid into the layer is wet.
- **Over wet, the soft brush does not build up tone:** over the wet of its own layer it takes off as much as it lays; tone grows only over dry (handbook, «Tone of the soft brush»).
- **Falloff over a large area** — patches of tone over wet in one layer, then blending of the joints: a wiped `мягкая` (`нагрузка=0`, color `None`), sweeps across the joint of 200–500 px, each next one in the opposite direction; the gauge — `х.переход(область)`: width 10–90 % and the step over 8 px (`примеры/переход.py`, handbook, «Blending»).
- **Load is how much paint per sweep.** A wide bristle brush of 100–140 px at 0.24–0.30 lets the ground through in the furrows; what covers over dry — `нагрузка_для(кисть, покрытие=…)`, by a series of rows — `план_покрытия`, by scattered sweeps over wet — by a probe.
- **The flat lays both a plane and a line:** laid flat — a trace of the full width, on its edge (`касание=0`) — a solid band as thick as the tuft, 1–2 mm for a 5 mm brush, wider with pressure; the thickness is the brush field `пучок_мм` (`примеры/ребро.py`, handbook, «Brush»).
- **Many lines in one sweep — a brush of tufts:** `веерная` lays per sweep 10–14 lines of 2–5 px of different length (a light hand — fewer, from the middle of the fan), with a jab — an arc of dashes; `растрёпанная` — 3–7 ragged bands with gaps, with a jab — patches of clumps with bare between them (foliage, tree crown). Neighboring sweeps with the same call come out as different layouts of lines; the mirror `х.однообразие` counts such sweeps by lines (handbook, «Brush» and «Recipes»).
- **The brush remembers dirt:** paint picked up from the canvas goes on with the brush until `к.вытереть()`. How much of its own paint is on it — it remembers only after `к.набрать(цвет, количество)`: otherwise the brush is full on every sweep.
- **Saving a stage:** `х.сохранить(f'этап{i}.npz')` — a correction of stage 5 starts from `Холст.загрузить('этап4.npz', журнал='лист.jsonl')`, not from zero: the journal is cut back to the place of saving and is written on; the cut lines — in `лист.jsonl.до_отката` (`.2`, … — the previous copy is not overwritten), in stderr — «журнал обрезан до этап4.npz: N строк после неё — в …». Only to look at a stage — `Холст.загрузить('этап4.npz')`: the journal is taken for reading (`х.однообразие()`, `х.виды`), it is not written.
- **Rebuild a sheet from the journal** — `python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --out лист.png`. Strokes in different places of the canvas the replay computes on cores by itself (`--cores N`, without the option — by the machine's cores up to 16; the result is bit for bit as on one, `--cores 1` — one core). The replay itself writes checkpoints into `лист.точки/`: before each stage, once every 5 minutes (`--every N` — also every N strokes) and at the end of each go; keeps the last checkpoint of each stage and up to 12 intermediate ones as a ladder — denser at the end of the journal, sparser further away, — no more than 2 GB per folder (`--checkpoints-max`), removes the excess itself (the far intermediate ones first); prints the progress and how much is left. It broke off — the same call with `--resume`: it continues from the nearest checkpoint, the result is bit for bit as from the start; a journal line is edited by `х.править` (a journal file — by `кисть.задним`, the row «Save, go back, edit» of the map) — it takes these checkpoints itself and rebuilds only what is affected, while a line corrected by hand `--resume` honestly lays anew from the checkpoint before it. Editing a recent stroke — from a checkpoint near it (measured on a 2480×2380 sheet, 30 goes of «appended — `--resume`»: an edit 40 strokes before the end — from a checkpoint 80–271 strokes before it, ~14 s), editing an old one — from a checkpoint further away (a thousand strokes from the end — ~1500 strokes before the edit, ~100 s).
- **Strokes of a stage or object** — `python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --list --stage 4 --object заливка`; as a picture — `--out заливка.png --stage 4 --object заливка`.

## 6. Where things are

- `СПРАВОЧНИК.md` — the fields of `мазок` and of the journal, brushes and their fields, technique recipes, replay and painting on, what the engine cannot do; `python -m кисть.справка <слово>`.

- Check that the engine is intact: from `движок/` — `python3 -m unittest discover -s тесты -t . -v` (system Python; slow — the files can be run in several processes); faster — `python -m pytest тесты -n 10 -q -m "not скорость"`, only in a `.venv` with `pytest` and `pytest-xdist`.

## Concepts

- **Canvas** — `Холст(ширина, высота, грунт=(150, 141, 126), px_на_мм=None, зерно=None, журнал=None, сид=0)`, px, y down. 4800×3000 in three layers — 1.35 GB. `px_на_мм` is needed by a brush in mm and by the grain (without it — `ValueError`); set it to 8.0. `журнал='лист.jsonl'` — the canvas itself writes the journal (section «Stroke journal»); `сид` — the seed of the sheet.
- **Layer** — named, created by the first stroke into it; the first one created is the lowest. A stroke mixes with the wet paint of its own layer; with the lower ones — only with `мешать_ниже=True`.
- **Wet and dry.** Fresh paint is wet. `подсушить(1.0)` — fully dry, `схватить(0.5)` — a skin, over it a stroke lies sharply. Time does not pass by itself: drying is done by whoever calls it.
- **Brush** — a preset from `пресеты/*.json` and the width of the trace. The brush remembers the paint picked up: the next stroke with the same object will start dirty, until `к.вытереть()`.
- **Path** — points `(x, y, нажим 0–1, dt_мс)`. `жест` and `путь_с_нажимом` build them from anchor points and set the pressure «entry — body — exit».
- **Color** — sRGB 0–255: `(r, g, b)`, a set `[((r, g, b), доля), …]` or `Краска(rgb, вид)`; or the name of a mix of the canvas palette (`х.палитра`, «Palette»).
- **Seed.** The same seed and the same history of the brush — the same pixels. There is no chance outside the seed.
- **Scale.** The physics is computed in mm and tuned to 8 px/mm; a canvas without `px_на_мм` is also 8 px/mm. A brush in mm (`ширина_мм=6` — 48 px at 8 px/mm, 24 px at 4) at 4 and 16 px/mm lays a sweep of the same width, length, film thickness and tone in mm: the film thickness, the furrow under a hair, the spacing and place of the hairs, the reserve of the tip, the reserve of paint in the brush hair, the skipping of a hair and the height of the relief for the light follow `px_на_мм`. Live paint (`набрать(цвет, количество)`) lasts for the same path in mm: `плоская` 6 mm with a full charge — 174 · 178 · 179 mm at 4 · 8 · 16 px/mm, `мягкая` 6 mm — 176 · 178 · 181, `плоская` 12 mm — 304 · 317 · 310; a trial in a ½ draft is right for the 1:1 sheet. A brush in px is px of the canvas: `ширина=80` at 16 px/mm is a brush of 5 mm. A draft at ½ is the same journal with `--scale 0.5`: the coordinates, the width and the hair spacing of the brush are multiplied, the sheet is the same in another resolution (a journal of 1113 wide sweeps: ½, enlarged twofold, against 1:1 — p95 over windows of 32 px 0.5 L without light, 1.1 with light; a journal of 4042 small sweeps — 1.4). A draft at ¼ — `--scale 0.25`: a sheet of 2480×2380 in 14 thousand strokes on one core — 81 s, at ½ — 137 s, 1:1 — 360 s (four times and 2.6 times faster; the hairs are the same, therefore not 16 times: a stroke costs its own even when small). A hair thinner than a pixel is not visible: at 4 px/mm the furrows are softer, at 16 there are more gaps between the strands of thin paint (1.2 % of the sweep at 4, 1.6 % at 8, 4.8 % at 16). **What holds px** (`кисть.масштаб.держит_px(кисть, нагрузка, px_на_мм)` — a tuple of words, at 8 px/mm empty): narrow — narrower than 24 px on this canvas or at 8 px/mm (the thresholds of a narrow brush are in px), soft (`кайма` > 0), dry (load `'сухая'`), the rim of thick paint (`'пастозная'` or a load from 0.6). On a canvas that is not 8 px/mm the first stroke with such a brush gives the hint **масштаб** (once per canvas, «Other public»); a draft with `--scale` < 1 names their number in the result («Running a replay»). **A large canvas at 8 px/mm** (800×500 mm — 6400×4000 px) is affordable on a weak machine: on 2 cores and 8 GB 3000 sweeps (1600 wide `плоская` 48–56, 600 over wet with `мешать_ниже`, 500 narrow and round 2.5–3 mm, 300 `мягкая`) — ~165 s, `х.png` — 5–7 s, `х.взгляд` of a region 1200×800 — 0.5 s, `х.сохранить` — 5–7.5 s (`.npz` 450 MB), `х.картинка()` whole — 3 s, peak memory — 4 GB for all processes; what is expensive is the strokes themselves, not the look («Speed and memory»). There is no need to go to 4 px/mm for the sake of speed.

## Calling from Python

```python
import sys; sys.path.insert(0, '<путь>/движок')
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, Краска, жест, путь_с_нажимом, нажим_вдоль
х = Холст(400, 240, грунт=(150, 141, 126), px_на_мм=8.0, журнал='лист.jsonl')   # журнал пишется сам
х.пометка('низ', этап=1, объект='фон')
плоская = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=48)
х.мазок(жест([[10, 24], [200, 28], [390, 24]], 48, 'остановка', сид=10, нажим=0.7), (70, 92, 84), плоская, слой='низ', сид=10, нагрузка='обычная')
плоская.вытереть()
х.подсушить(1.0)
мягкая = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/мягкая.json', ширина_мм=6)
х.мазок(путь_с_нажимом([[40, 170], [200, 180], [360, 165]], 48, сид=31, нажим=0.5), Краска((70, 34, 22), 'лессировочная'), мягкая, слой='лессировка', сид=31, нагрузка='протирка')
х.png('лист.png', свет=True)
```

### `Холст.мазок(точки, цвет, кисть, слой='основа', сид=None, …)`

Answers with the rectangle `(x0, y0, x1, y1)` that it wrote, with a measurement of the trace — `о.цвет`, `о.закрыто`, `о.просветы`, `о.ширина`, `о.сырое`, `о.разрывы`, `о.край` («Eyes», «Stroke response») — or with `None`. On cores (by default the canvas computes strokes on the machine's cores, «Speed and memory», «Cores») the stroke finishes computing later than the call, and the response is deferred: `изм.взять()` will finish computing this stroke and give the same response or `None`; as a tuple it behaves the same (`tuple(изм)`, `изм[0]`, `x0, y0, x1, y1 = изм`, `bool(изм)`, `изм == (…)`), the measurement fields are the same (`изм.цвет` will finish computing the stroke; the numbers are as on one core). One difference: `изм is None` will not work with a deferred response — check for an empty stroke with `not изм` or `изм.взять() is None`.

| Parameter | Journal | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| `точки` | `"core"` (`"ctrl"`, `"points"`) | `(x, y, нажим, dt_мс[, наклон_°, азимут_рад, поворот_°])`; only `(x, y)` — the pressure will be built by itself. One point — a jab |
| `цвет` | `"color"`, `"colors"` | `(r, g, b)`, a set `[(rgb, доля), …]`, `Краска(rgb, вид)`; `None` — the paint charged with `к.набрать(цвет)`; with `нагрузка=0` (a wiped brush) a color is not needed — `None` even without charging: the brush has no paint of its own, and the color of the charge, if there was one, does not go into the trace |
| `кисть` | `"brush"` (`"preset"`, `"size"`) | a `Кисть` object, required; in the journal — by the name `"к1"` and a line `"def"` with all the fields |
| `слой` | `"layer"` | layer name; a new one lies on top of the former ones |
| `сид` | `"seed"` | an integer; `None` — from the seed of the sheet, the stage, the object and the points of the stroke (`х.сид_мазка`), not from the number of the stroke |
| `нагрузка` | `"load"` | how much paint is on the brush: `'протирка'` (0.10), `'обычная'` (0.28), `'пастозная'` (0.90), `'сухая'` (half-empty, the trace breaks), a number — the film thickness at a width of 40 px; `0` — a wiped soft brush (blending); `None` — as the brush has it |
| `раскладка` | `"layout"` | how the paints of a set lie: `'полосы'` (across the brush, the order is random), `'ряд'` (in order), `'смесь'`, `'кончик'` (the second paint at one edge) |
| `мешать_ниже` | `"mix_below"` | to mix also with the wet paint of the layers below |
| `касание` | `"touch"` | flat: `1` — laid flat, a trace of the full width even at light pressure; `0` — on its edge: the brush is turned edge-on to the sweep, the trace is a solid band as thick as the tuft (brush field `пучок_мм`; `плоская` 5 mm — 1.25–1.5 mm at pressure 0.3–0.9, 10 mm — 2.25–3.0 mm), wider with pressure and tilt of the handle; between 0 and 1 — a rotation by (1 − touch)·90°. A jab does not depend on the touch |
| `по_гребням` | `"ridges"` | 0–1: with a light hand to lay onto the tops of the ridges of the paint lying there, the furrows are empty |
| `ровно` | `"level"` | 0–1: flowing opaque paint closes into an even layer, the body is without furrows |
| `разбавитель` | `"medium"` | 0–1: liquid paint (oil, solvent) — the film is thinner, more transparent, flows without furrows (non-opaque too), is hole-free over the grain, the dry breakaway stretches longer; thick — without the field. Neither with `'сухая'` nor with `0` |
| `запас` | `"reserve"` | >0: dry breakaway — paint for this many brush widths, the sweep breaks into islands toward the end |
| `волокна` | `"fibers"` | 0–1, only with `нагрузка='сухая'`: the edge breaks into fibers along the sweep |
| `полосы` | `"stripe"`, `"stripe_len"` | a set of thick paints in pieces: `(ширина полосы, длина куска)` px, any of them — `None` |
| `нагрузка_вдоль` | `"load_along"` | release of paint along the sweep, soft brush only |
| `ломаная` | `"points"` | `True` — the points are the raw path of the hand: a corner stays a corner, there is no entry and no exit; pressure is needed in the points |
| `непрозрачность` | `"opacity"` | mixing in as a film; a pure glaze — `Краска(…, 'лессировочная')` (turbidity 0: does not lighten the dark, L 23 → 21–27); `'прозрачная'` (turbidity 0.03) over dark lightens: L 23 → 40–54 |
| `нажим_руки` | `"hand"` (`"pressure_along"`) | `True` — the pressure of the points is led by the hand: the flat laid flat touches with a width according to pressure; set it together with `нажим_вдоль` |
| `этап`, `объект`, `намерение` | `"stage"`, `"object"`, `"intent"` | only into the journal: stage, object, what the stroke is for; stage and object without them — from the last `х.пометка` |

A stroke that left no trace is a warning `ПустойМазок` with the cause, by where the stroke ended: `нагрузка 0` (a brush without paint, and there is no wet under it — named first), `нажим 0`, `ход вне холста` (the trace of the brush is wholly past the edge, the sweep is farther than the brush width from the edge), `нажим до … — ниже порога касания`, `краски не легло` (the reserve ran out). A strike whose trace lay wholly past the edge, while the sweep came to the edge closer than the brush width, is silent: going past the edge of a piece is a usual thing. The stroke goes into the journal all the same. The word sounds on the first stroke of each kind of cause, after that — a count: `х.пустые` → `{'ход вне холста': 312, …}`; the replay at the end writes a line «без следа: …».

### Path of the hand

- `жест(ctrl, размер, вид='подъём', rng=None, нажим=0.85, скорость=1.0, вход=None, сид=None)` — the end of the sweep: `'подъём'` (the pressure comes off over 2–4 widths, the end is a wedge), `'отрыв'` (a breakaway over a quarter of the width, the end is of the full width), `'остановка'` (stopped, pressed down, lifted — a blunt end), `'смахивание'` (the end is led aside in an arc).
- `путь_с_нажимом(ctrl, размер, rng=None, нажим=0.85, скорость=1.0, вход=None, сход=None, сид=None)` — the same with the lengths of the entry and exit in px.
- `сид=` — a number (as the first choice) or `rng` — a numpy generator (`np.random.default_rng(…)`). With a seed the stroke's path is its own: a stroke can be inserted, removed or moved in Python, and the paths of the others will not shift; in the journal it is three times shorter (`"path_seed"` against `"path_rng"`). One `rng` for a whole stage shifts the paths of all the strokes that follow from it, as soon as a stroke is inserted above. A seed gives the same points that the replay builds from the line `{"ctrl": …, "size": размер, "end": вид, "seed": сид}`. The journal writes such a stroke as anchor points (`"ctrl"`, `"size"`, `"end"`, `"pressure"`, `"entry"` and the path seed — `"path_seed"` or the generator state `"path_rng"`), and not as hundreds of core points; the replay builds the same points bit for bit. If the points were corrected after the call (`т[:, 2] *= 0.5`, `нажим_вдоль`, a slice) or `скорость`, `сход` are not the defaults — the journal writes `"core"`, as is.
- `размер` — the brush width in px. `вход` — the length of the pressure build-up, px: 3 — a flat butt end, more — a soft landing; by default 0.5–1.7 of the width. **A short sweep** (up to 1 brush width, entry and exit not given): the hand sets the brush down, drags and takes it off — entry 0.12 of the width, exit of `'подъём'` 0.25; the trace is of the full brush width from the first pixel (both with the end `'остановка'` and with `вход=5` — the flat 44 px with a sweep of 22 px: 0.94–1.03 of the width in 30 out of 30, there is no slit down to the ground; with a sweep of up to 1.5 widths the strands do not go aside right from the moment of the first touch, there is no thin place as a streak — the brush is full), the length of the trace is the length of the sweep (the flat 19 px with a sweep of 19 px at pressure 1 — a patch of 20×19 px, it was 16×16 as a spindle). From 1 to 1.5 widths — smoothly to the usual entry and exit; from 1.5 widths the sweep is as before bit for bit: the landing and the lift are visible in the silhouette, a sweep of 2 widths lays a trace 1.5–1.8 long (if a bar is needed — `вход=3` and the end `'отрыв'`). Your own `вход=`, `сход=` remain as given. The trace is narrower and shorter than the rectangle «path × width»: `жест(…, 'отрыв', вход=2.5)` of 2–8 widths, load 0.15, covers (coverage from half of the greatest) 0.75–0.87 of its area for `плоская` 24 and 0.77–0.98 for `мягкая` 14–24 (4 seeds, the soft 14 with a sweep of 2 widths is the lowest of all); `мягкая` with a short sweep — the path is longer by 0.45 of the width at each end: 0.88–1.0; for `плоская` what is missing is at the sides, not at the ends.
- `нажим_вдоль(точки, [[доля пути 0–1, множитель], …])` — pressure along the sweep, straight-line between the nodes.
- `тычок(x, y, нажим=0.85, угол=None)` — a touch on the spot: an imprint of the tuft for a brush of any width. Round — a circle of the tuft with a diameter of the trace width at this pressure, the edge is a fringe of tips, without facets or slits (22 px: pressure 0.3 — ~9 × 10 px, 0.6 — ~19 × 21, 0.85 — ~22 × 22, 1.0 — ~25 × 25; 32–64 px at pressure 0.85 — axes 1.0–1.05 of the width; at light pressure the short strands do not reach — the edge is uneven, the patch is no smaller than 0.8 of the width at 0.6; a narrow round 6–14 px — a dot of its own width at any angle: 6 px — ~6 × 7, 10 — ~10 × 11, 14 — ~14 × 15 at pressure 0.85); flat, narrow, soft — a bar of the full brush width, along `угла` 0.45–0.85 of the width (pressure 0.3–1); flat and narrow 4–12 px — an imprint of the brush's size (4 px — ~4 × 4, 8 — ~8 × 7, 12 — ~12 × 10 at pressure 0.85), on its edge (`касание=0`) — the same: the brush stands on its whole butt end. `угол` — the direction of the brush, degrees from the x axis (the wide side of the flat is across it). The same in the journal — `{"ctrl": [[x, y]], "pressure": нажим, "angle": угол}`; without pressure and angle a jab is `[(x, y)]`, in the journal `"ctrl"`, `"points"` or `"core"` with one point `[[x, y]]`, the pressure is built by the core. An example is below the list.
- `ломаная(точки, шаг=0.5)` — to fill in the raw points of the hand every half pixel in a straight line: no spline, no entry and no exit.
- `кисть.повтор.точки_журнала(points, масштаб=1, отставание=0)` — a line's `"points"` into core points; `х.мазок(точки_журнала(points, 1, к.отставание * ширина), …)` lays the same as the journal (`отставание` — only for the flat with an azimuth column).
- `участки_касания(точки, профиль)` — where the pressure multiplier is 0, the brush is above the canvas: a list of pieces, each is its own `мазок`; `None` if there are no zeros.

```python
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, тычок
х = Холст(120, 80)
к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/круглая.json', ширина=14)
х.мазок(тычок(40, 40, 0.8, 30), (240, 235, 215), к, слой='верх', сид=1, нагрузка='пастозная')   # = {"ctrl": [[40, 40]], "pressure": 0.8, "angle": 30}
х.мазок([(80, 40)], (230, 240, 220), к, слой='верх', сид=2)                                    # = {"points": [[80, 40]]}
```

### Brush

`Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/имя.json', ширина=…)` or `ширина_мм=…`; any field of the preset — by a named parameter: `Кисть.из_файла(…, разброс_тона=0.3)`. `к.с(поле=…)` — a clean copy with a replacement, `к.копия()` — a copy with what is on the brush, `к.вытереть()`, `к.промыть()`, `к.набрать(цвет, количество=None, раскладка='полосы')` — how much paint is on the brush and how it is spent, section «Paint on the brush». A field is also edited directly: `к.разброс_тона = 0.3` — the journal will write the brush anew; a typo (`к.разброс_тонa = 0.3`) — `AttributeError: кисть: нет поля разброс_тонa, может быть разброс_тона?`, and not a new attribute that the stroke does not see.

| Preset | What it is | When to take it |
|---|---|---|
| `плоская` | flat bristle in a ferrule, 48 px | mass, fill, strike; overlapping sweeps grow together into a patch; on its edge (`касание=0`) — a line and a rim as thick as the tuft, 1–2 mm |
| `плоская_узкая` | bristle with a sharp tip, 14 px | line, mark 3–16 px; the trace follows the pressure |
| `круглая` | round bristle, 20 px | dot, rounded strike, line |
| `мягкая` | squirrel, kolinsky, 48 px, edge band 0.30 | the edge fades to nothing: shadow, joint, blending |
| `сухая` | half-dry flat, 36 px | dry brush, scumble (with `нагрузка='сухая'`) |
| `лайнер` | long soft hair 2–4 px | a line of even width, paint for ~300 px of the sweep, toward the end thinner and paler |
| `веерная` | fan bristle, 80 px: brush hair in one row in sparse tufts, the tips in an arc (`просветы` 0.55, `в_пучке` 1, `веер` 0.18) | grass, foliage, fur, hair in one sweep: a drag — a tuft of lines of 2–5 px of different length and thickness (pressure 0.35 — 5–10 lines from the middle of the fan, 0.7 — 10–14 over the full width), a jab — an arc of dashes |
| `растрёпанная` | old flat bristle, 48 px: the brush hair sticks out in clumps of different length and stiffness (`просветы` 0.35, `в_пучке` 3) | bark, dry grass, ragged texture: a drag — 3–7 bands of 4–14 px with bare gaps, a jab — patches of clumps with bare between them (foliage, tree crown) |

### Paint

`Краска((r, g, b), вид)`: `'кроющая'` (as a plain color), `'полукроющая'`, `'прозрачная'`, `'лессировочная'`, `'медиум'` (without color, below) or turbidity 0–1. How much of what is below is visible is decided by the film thickness, that is, the load. `'лессировочная'` — turbidity 0, a pure glaze: the film does not cover with its own color, it only darkens what lies there by transmitted light, it does not lighten the dark (the flat 24 px over L 23: 21–27; over L 200 — 188–192). `'прозрачная'` — turbidity 0.03: its turbid share covers with its own color, over dark it lightens (L 23 → 40–54 with the same green (118, 228, 117)). A sweep over a wet film of turbidity 0 picks it up and mixes it, as over a wet one of low turbidity. Lay a glaze and a veil as their own layer over dried paint: the relief of the dried paint under them stands (the brush levels only the wet — «Drying»). An `.npz` of the former delivery, where the kind `'лессировочная'` did not exist yet, carries in a wet film of turbidity 0 (`Краска(rgb, 0.0)`) a black color: a sweep over it will pick up black — rebuild the sheet from the journal by a replay («Running a replay») or lay the film again.

**Thinner** is a property of the paint in a stroke, separate from the load: `х.мазок(…, разбавитель=0.7)` (`"medium"`, 0–1). Liquid paint lies thinly, without relief, more transparently, flows into the grain of the canvas and stretches longer; thick paint (without the field) holds the relief. At 1 the film is twice as thin, turbidity ×0.4, the furrows close as with `ровно=1` (also for semi-opaque and transparent paint, to which `ровно` does not apply), the reserve of the dry breakaway is twice as long. The trace of the brush hair holds up to 0.5–0.7; from 0.9 the body of the sweep is smooth, without a trace of the brush. The trace of the brush in the body of a sweep is two different things: the furrows of the relief (visible with light) and the strands of tone (`разброс_тона`, visible without light too). `плоская` 48, load 0.6, over the ground (150, 141, 126), `х.борозды` of the body, 3 seeds: without thinner with light σ 7–10 L at any `разброс_тона`, with thinner 0.3 — 2–3 L (at `разброс_тона` 1 — 4.5); without light — strands: `разброс_тона` 0.12 — ~1 L, 0.5 — 2.5, 1 — 5, thinner 0.3 hardly touches them, 0.7 — twofold. The edge of the film is a slope of the relief with a length in mm, and not a step of one pixel: for thick paint 0.125 mm (sharp, the largest jump at the edge over dry 0.88 with a body of 1.05), for thinner 0.4 — 1.0 mm (0.24), 0.7 — 1.3 mm (0.11 against the former 0.60), 1 — 1.5 mm (0.06); at 16 px/mm the edge is of the same length in mm, the jump per pixel is half as large. Over wet paint of its own layer — the same slope: a sweep of 0.4 over wet gives at the edge a jump of 0.111 per pixel (smaller than over dry, — there is no light thread under the light along the contour of the sweep), the wet paint lying under the sweep is displaced along the same slope, without a groove at the edge. The pigment of the edge lies as it lay: the slope is in the relief, not in the color. A fill by rows with the flat 100 px, load 0.9:

**A flowing layer over dry closes the relief.** A sweep with `разбавитель` or `ровно` over dried paint — of its own layer and of the layers below — does not repeat its relief with a film of even thickness, but flows into the hollows: in a depression the layer is thicker, on a ridge thinner, the top is more even than the bottom. What lies there does not change, there is the same amount of paint, the film does not flow beyond its own trace; the pigment (color, coverage) lies as it lay — it is the oil that flows. The strength is from the film thickness (thinner than 0.05 hardly flows, from 0.15 flows freely) and the fluidity (`разбавитель`, `ровно`: 0.4 — less, 1 — fully). A ridge higher than the film stands: from an impasto sweep the film comes down to its foot, flooding the furrows of the brush hair, while the body and the rim remain. Thick paint (without `разбавитель` and `ровно`) and a half-empty brush repeat the relief of what lies there and add their own. Over the wet of its own layer — as it was.

**Medium** — `МЕДИУМ` (`from кисть import МЕДИУМ`; the same as `Краска(вид='медиум')`, in the journal `"paint": "медиум"`): oil or varnish by brush, a film without pigment. It does not change the colors of what lies there (without light — less than 1 L), it has no undermixing of strands (`разброс_тона`); it lays only thickness — and closes the relief, like any flowing film. Lay it with `разбавитель=1` with a clean brush (a dirty one will bring its own color). The gloss and saturation of what is covered are given by the output key `поверхность=` («Output»), medium does not change them.

### Paint on the brush

How much paint is on the brush is decided by the charge. `к.набрать(цвет)` without an amount and a sweep with a color in the call — the brush is full on every sweep: five sweeps without a recharge are equal. `к.набрать(цвет, количество)` — the paint is **live**: `количество` is the share of a full brush, greater than 0 and up to 1 (1 — the brush is full; it does not take more — `ValueError`). A full brush is one and the same volume of paint, whatever sweep and seed it is laid with; the fresh brush of a sweep with a color is charged with the chance of the sweep around the same volume (±20 %), therefore the first sweep on a full charge is close to it, and not equal. A sweep without a color (`х.мазок(точки, None, к)`) spends it by the film laid — as much leaves the brush hair as lay on the canvas — the next one starts from the remainder: a series on one charge comes out unequal by itself — the first sweep is dense, the later ones grow poorer and break along the brush hair, in the same way as the end of a long sweep. There is no chance in this: the same series — the same pixels. The name of a palette mix — through the canvas: `х.набрать(к, 'умбра', 0.7)` (or `х.палитра.набрать`).

| Call | What it does |
|---|---|
| `к.набрать(цвет, количество)` | the brush is charged anew to a share of a full one; the former remainder of paint is replaced, what was picked up on the tips (dirt) stays |
| `к.осталось`, `о.осталось` | how much paint is in the brush hair, the share of a full brush 0–1 — on the brush and in the response of a sweep; `None` — the paint is not live. `print(о)` — «на кисти осталось 0.31» |
| `к.вытереть()` | against a rag: there is nothing picked up on the tips; with live paint it is taken off too — from the tips all of it, in the brush hair a twentieth part remains: the next sweep is a rub (covered 0.18 against 0.81 for the flat 6 mm: the tip is fed from the brush hair in proportion to the remainder) |
| `к.промыть()` | the brush is clean and empty: a sweep without a color — only after `набрать` |
| a sweep with a color in the call | with a full brush of this color, as always; does not spend live paint |
| `к.хватит(нажим=None, доля=0.5, нагрузка=None, ход_мм=40, разбавитель=None, холст=None, px_на_мм=None)` | for how many mm of path what is on the brush now lasts — before laying, by a trial on its own test bench; the brush, the canvas and the journal are not touched (below, «Enough for N mm») |

While the brush is in the air, paint from the brush hair comes up to the tip (the volume is the same: as much left the brush hair as came to the tip): each sweep starts denser than the previous one ended — and ends earlier. A brush with empty brush hair lays only what remained on the tips. A sweep with an empty brush is not `None`: there is a response, `закрыто` 0.00 (from a clot at lift-off — up to 0.003), `осталось` 0.0; an empty brush is recognized by `о.осталось` and `о.закрыто`. `None` and the word `ПустойМазок` are for a sweep that did not touch the canvas at all. Dry breakaway within a sweep is in `о.разрывы` with the cause `'краска'`. Wet paint picked up goes on with the brush, as before (dirt on the tips).

**Running out — with a dry brush over the full width.** The brush hair of the brush is one ferrule: the paint of the reserve spreads over the neighboring ridges, and a pressed ridge, giving off its own, takes from a neighbor that touches more weakly; the tip is fed from the brush hair fully while there is more than a fifth part of a full brush in it, after that — in proportion to the remainder. An emptying brush lays a ragged dry band over the full width, and not two threads at the edges with an empty middle: in the running out (`мягкая` and `плоская` 6 mm, sweeps of 80 mm on one charge) the middle of the trace lays 1.1–1.4 of what the edges do (it was 0.4–0.8 for the soft). The edge of the trace — a thread of tips at one side remains here and there. The example and the gauge are `тесты/test_исход_Долгф.py` (there too, a dot of `круглая` 5–6 px by a jab — solid, and an almost empty `мягкая` at the edge of the layer's paint — without gray).

**The paint lasts for a path, and not for a number of sweeps** — by number too: one full charge lays one and the same paint on the canvas, however the path is cut. **Enough for N mm — before laying**. `п = к.хватит(холст=х)` — how many mm of path sweeps of `ход_мм` (40) will go with this brush, with what is on it now (the remainder, the dirt on the tips), without a recharge, before the sweep's `закрыто` falls below `доля` (0.5) of what is covered by the first sweep of a fresh full charge of the same paint, load and pressure. The response is a dictionary: `'мм'` (the mean of 12 series), `'ходов'` (the same in sweeps of `ход_мм`), `'разброс_мм'` (σ of the series: one sweep is not like another), `'закрыто_свежей'`, `'порог'`, `'закрыто_ходов'` (covered by the sweeps of the series in order, the mean of the series — it is visible where the trace will start to grow poorer), `'осталось'`, `'ход_мм'`, `'px_на_мм'`, `'нажим'`, `'сидов'`; `'больше'` — in words, if the brush has not run out within 2000 mm. `нажим=None` — as a sweep by points `(x, y)` (the hand by itself, body 0.85), as a number — the path `путь_с_нажимом(…, нажим=)`; `нагрузка=` — as for a stroke (None — that of the brush). **The scale is required** — `холст=` or `px_на_мм=`: the test bench lays the sweeps in canvas px. The path in mm does not depend on px (`плоская` 6 mm on a full charge: 174 · 178 · 179 mm at 4 · 8 · 16 px/mm), except for a brush that is narrow on this canvas (narrower than 24 px, its physics holds px): `круглая` 3 mm — 51 mm at 4 px/mm and 132 at 8 and 16. 0.4–4 s per call at 4 px/mm, 6–20 s at 8, at 16 — tens of seconds (`плоская` 12 mm — up to 70 s). The test bench is `кисть/хватит.py`: a smooth light ground, straight sweeps in rows, the wet paint of the previous sweep does not lie under the next one; the path over the grain and over wet — by a trial (`х.проба`). A brush without live paint (without an amount) — `ValueError`: it is full on every sweep.

Numbers of the test bench (40 mm, share 0.5; the measurement is the same sweeps on a canvas with 12 seeds, the deviation is less than 1 %): `плоская` 6 mm — charge 1: 174 mm at 4 px/mm, 174 at 8, 174 at 16; charge 0.5: 98, 99 and 99. `мягкая` 6 mm — 172, 174 and 174; 101, 102 and 102. `круглая` 3 mm (narrow at 4 px/mm) — 52, 131 and 129; 24, 76 and 74. The remainder after sweeps agrees (8 px/mm): `плоская` 6 mm full — 174, after one sweep of 40 mm — 129, after two — 85; wiped — 13.

The forecasts know the remainder: for a brush with live paint `нагрузка_для(к, …, длина=мм хода)` answers with the same number (it is the one to lay with) with the fields `.хватит_мм`, `.на_ход` (True — the remainder is enough, at this load, for a sweep of length `длина`) and `.хватит` (the whole response of `к.хватит`); `план_покрытия(к, …, длина=мм ряда)` — with a dictionary with the keys `'хватит_мм'`, `'хватит'` and `'рядов_до_добора'`. The canvas px is `px_на_мм=` (without it 8). For a brush without live paint the responses are as before, bit for bit.

```python
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, нагрузка_для, план_покрытия
х = Холст(360, 120, грунт=(235, 232, 225), px_на_мм=4.0)
к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина_мм=6)
к.набрать((60, 40, 30), 0.5)                            # полкисти живой краски
п = к.хватит(холст=х)                                   # ничего не кладёт, кисть та же
print(п['мм'], п['ходов'], п['закрыто_ходов'])          # 98.2 2.46 [0.7998, 0.6713, 0.3819, 0.1557]
н = нагрузка_для(к, покрытие=0.7, px_на_мм=4.0, длина=60)
print(н, н.хватит_мм, н.на_ход)                         # 0.15829 165.9 True — тоньше плёнка, путь длиннее
пл = план_покрытия(к, 0.9, px_на_мм=4.0, длина=40)
print(пл['нагрузка'], пл['рядов_до_добора'])            # 0.30191 2 — после двух рядов по 40 мм добрать
for i in range(int(п['ходов']) + 1):                     # ходы по 40 мм, пока хватает; потом добор
    if к.хватит(холст=х)['мм'] < 40:
        к.набрать((60, 40, 30), 0.5)
    х.мазок([(20, 15 + 25 * i), (180, 15 + 25 * i)], None, к, сид=3)
```

### Palette

The palette of mixes is the named colors of the canvas: `х.палитра({'охра свет': (214, 168, 96), 'умбра': цвет(70, 0.04, 60)})` creates or edits mixes all at once, `х.палитра.добавить(имя, цвет)` — one. A mix is one paint: `(r, g, b)`, `Краска(rgb, вид)`, `цвет(L, C, h)`, `смешать_srgb(a, b, доля)`; a set of paints is not a mix (`ValueError`). A stroke takes a color by name: `цвет='охра свет'` in `мазок`, `проба`, `перебор`, `варианты`, `прямоугольник_мазка`; a list of names is a set of paints on the brush, like a list of colors (`['охра свет', 'умбра']` — equally, `[('охра свет', 0.7), ('умбра', 0.3)]` — by shares; a name and rgb in one list — allowed), with the same `раскладка`. A brush charges with a mix like this: `к.набрать(х.палитра['умбра'])` or `х.палитра.набрать(к, 'умбра')`; `к.набрать('умбра')` — `ValueError` (the name is known by the canvas, not the brush). If there is no such name — `KeyError` with the nearest names: `палитра: замеса «охра свт» нет; ближайшие: 'охра свет'`. `х.палитра['умбра']` — `Замес` (`Краска` with `имя`), `х.палитра.имена`, `len`, `in`; `print(х.палитра)` — name, rgb, L C h of each.

The name changes only the journal, not the pixels: a stroke by name lies bit for bit as with the same color directly, at the same speed. The shade is given to the stroke by the brush (dirt, `разброс_тона`, the wet under the sweep), the palette does not change the color. Journal: a mix is the line `{"palette": "умбра", "color": [92, 64, 44]}` (stage and object from the label), a stroke and a charging with a mix — with the field `"swatch"` beside the color. The replay takes the color of a mix from the palette line above the stroke: an edit of the mix's line in the journal and `--resume` rebuild its strokes (from the nearest checkpoint before the edit), the others lie bit for bit. `х.палитра.добавить('умбра', …)` in the middle of a sheet — the strokes after it with the new color, those that lay earlier — with the former one. A rollback of a snapshot brings the palette back too; `х.сохранить` and `Холст.загрузить` keep it with the canvas.

A color for each sweep (`(r, g, b)` by a formula from the reference at the point) remains allowed, but the sheet is then a rendered one: each stroke is of its own color, there are no decisions «where which mix». The mirror shows this: `х.однообразие()` — «цветов N разных, на глаз K; замесов M, не с палитры P %», by zones — the mixes of each object, for places — the share not from the palette (if there is a palette on the sheet); if of the first hundred sweeps of an object with paint, 80 or more are not from the palette, and their colors are different — the hint **цвет формулой** on the hundredth sweep.

**Draft from a reference** — `ч = х.палитра_с(область, образец, n=12, путь=)`: n mixes from the reference in the region (the reference is like the target of the eyes: a picture in canvas coordinates, a path, a canvas; a photo of another size — `уложить`). The response is a dictionary «name → (r, g, b)», the light ones first, names like `'средний серо-охристый 111'` (the number is L); `ч.доли` — the share of the area of each, `ч.прищур_dL` — the mean difference of tone between the reference and the same reference tiled with the nearest mix, in a squint (like `х.виды`), `ч.замощение`, `ч.лист` (`путь` — a PNG «reference | tiling | swatches» 1:1). It creates nothing and lays nothing. The mixes are k means by area, the start is steps of lightness equally from the dark edge of the reference to the light one: a dark and a light patch of small area remain mixes of their own; there is no mix without area (there may be fewer than n). On the pilots' photos at n = 12 `прищур_dL` is 1.4–2.0 L, computing is ~1 s for 1200×800. Small bright patches (a red window on blue) do not get into the 12 mixes — add one of your own. A mix of the draft is the color of a mass in a squint: a motley mass (small patches of different colors interspersed) gives their mixture, the chroma of the mix is 20–37 % lower than that of its patches; the pure paints of such a mass — as mixes of their own. A draft is edited like a dictionary (warmer, purer, remove the superfluous) and created: `х.палитра(ч)`.

```python
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, жест
х = Холст(600, 300, грунт=(166, 146, 129), px_на_мм=8.0, журнал='палитра.jsonl')
х.палитра({'охра свет': (214, 168, 96), 'серо-сиреневый 2': (132, 120, 150), 'умбра': (92, 64, 44)})
к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=40)
for i in range(30):
    х.мазок(жест([[20 + 60 * (i % 9), 30 + 25 * (i // 9)], [70 + 60 * (i % 9), 34 + 25 * (i // 9)]], 40, 'остановка', сид=i),
            ['охра свет', 'серо-сиреневый 2', 'умбра'][i % 3], к, сид=i, нагрузка='обычная')
х.палитра.добавить('небо', (170, 190, 210))                     # пополнение по ходу — строка журнала
х.мазок(жест([[20, 200], [560, 205]], 40, 'отрыв', сид=40), ['охра свет', 'небо'], к, сид=40, нагрузка='пастозная')   # смесь двух замесов на кисти
print(х.однообразие())                                          # … цветов 4 разных, на глаз 4; замесов 4, не с палитры 0 %
```

### Drying, trial, rollback, removal, output

- `х.подсушить(доля=1.0, слой=None, область=None)`, `х.схватить(доля=0.5, слой=None, область=None)`; `область = (x0, y0, x1, y1)`. Dried paint is hard: a sweep of the brush over it does not level its relief and takes nothing off it — it lies on top, the ridges of a dry impasto sweep stand (under an almost empty brush — as they were). The brush levels only the wet paint of its own layer; a skin (`схватить`) and what is partly dried — more weakly, in proportion to how much wet there still is in them. Remove a dry relief with a flowing layer («Paint», closing), cover it with an impasto sweep or cut it off with a knife (`х.прижать`, below); bring wetness back to a dry place with `х.размочить`.
- Trial of a piece: `с = х.снимок(кисти=(к,))` → strokes → `х.откатить(с)` — bit for bit as it was; a rollback to a younger snapshot throws out the records of the older ones.
- One stroke as a trial: `изм, картинка = х.проба(точки, цвет, к, сид=…, слой=…, свет=True)` — lay, take the picture, roll back; the brush does not get dirty. `изм` — the response of the stroke with a measurement (`изм.просветы`, `изм.цвет`, «Stroke response»). `картинка` is not the canvas, but only the rectangle of the stroke `изм = (x0, y0, x1, y1)` (with margins for the rim), linear light; the place of the canvas point (x, y) in it is `картинка[y - изм[1], x - изм[0]]`. If a whole piece of the canvas is needed — `х.проба(…, область=(x0, y0, x1, y1))`: the picture of this region, `картинка[y - y0, x - x0]`. If the stroke did not touch the canvas — `(None, None)`.
- Computing in processes: `у = х.участок(прямоугольник)`, `х.вписать(у)` (subareas can be pickled; a subarea computes by itself, on one core; the fields of a subarea are in tiles, the memory is only its tiles). A subarea painted in another process does not carry a journal — for attempts in processes there is `варианты`.
- **Variants of a piece.** `вв = х.варианты(кусок, n, область, ядра=None)` — the function `кусок(участок, i)` lays strokes on a copy of the subarea `область = (x0, y0, x1, y1)`; n copies are computed at once in processes (no more than the canvas cores and 16), each one from the canvas as it was at the call. The canvas and the brushes are not touched. The response is n variants: `в.картинка` (linear float32 of the region, like `х.картинка()` with light by default; into PNG — `из_линейного`, example below), `в.ответ` (what `кусок` returned: numbers, strings, arrays, dictionaries), `в.номер`, `в.область`, `в.слова` (the warnings of the piece — they will sound at `взять`), `в.однообразие` (the mirror of the piece's strokes, like `х.однообразие`; the numbers are the lines of the piece from 1; a canvas without a journal has it too). Choose by eye (crops side by side), then `х.взять(вв[k])`: the variant lies into the canvas and into the journal with its own strokes — the canvas, journal, brushes and seeds are the same bytes that `кусок(х, k)`, painted directly, would give; a replay of the journal — the same `.npz`. The others are forgotten. In what the variants differ is decided by the piece by `i`: another object of the label (the seeds of the strokes are from the seed of the sheet, the stage, the object and the points), other points or colors; identical pictures — the warning `варианты: одинаковые картинки у вариантов …`. A stroke whose frame (the trace with margins, `прямоугольник_мазка`) goes outside the region — `ValueError` with a hint before computing: beside the computing of the variants goes a scouting — all n pieces without computing the strokes (a stroke deep in the region is not computed at all, at the rim — only by its frame, like `прямоугольник_мазка`); the first stroke wider than the region — an error in fractions of a second (a piece of 2500 strokes, the last one past the rim: 0.7 s, by computing it was 44 s), the other variants are shut down; an error of a variant during computing shuts down the others in the same way. A piece that decides by the canvas (`у.взгляд`, `у.картинка`, the response of a stroke) sees in the scouting the subarea without its own strokes: its stroke wider than the region will be reported by the computing of the variant. Take the region with room for the stroke's margin. Drying and setting in a piece — only with `область=` inside the piece's region, `снять` — before `варианты` or after `взять`. Between `варианты` and `взять` do not touch the canvas (a stroke, drying, a rollback — `взять` will say «холст менялся») and do not charge brushes. 8 variants of a piece 800×600 with 60 strokes each on 8 cores — 1.1 s (one on one core — 0.9 s), memory — +110 MB; on a canvas of 2480×2380 each call of `варианты` costs, over and above the piece, ~0.4 s — give the pieces seconds of work, and not one stroke each.

```python
from PIL import Image
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, путь_с_нажимом
from кисть.краска import из_линейного
х = Холст(500, 400, ядра=2)
к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=18.0)

def кусок(у, i):                                   # вариант i: свой объект пометки — свои сиды мазков
    у.пометка('проба', этап=1, объект=f'проба {i}')
    for j in range(4):
        у.мазок(путь_с_нажимом([(150 + 40 * j, 160), (190 + 40 * j, 175)], 18.0, сид=j, нажим=0.7), (70 + 30 * i, 110, 170), к, слой='проба')
    у.подсушить(1.0, область=(140, 140, 360, 200))  # сушка в куске — только с область= внутри области куска
    return {'i': i}

вв = х.варианты(кусок, 3, (100, 100, 400, 260))    # три попытки разом
for в in вв:                                         # кропы в PNG — смотреть глазами рядом друг с другом
    Image.fromarray((из_линейного(в.картинка) * 255 + 0.5).astype('uint8')).save(f'вариант_{в.номер}.png')
х.взять(вв[2])                                       # выбранная — в холст и журнал, как если бы писалась прямо
```
- **Variants on disk — choosing in another launch of Python.** The script has ended, the pictures are open, the decision is made — and the process with the variants is already gone. `вв = х.варианты(кусок, n, область, в_папку='варианты/фигура')` — the same as without `в_папку`, and each variant to disk as well: `<в_папку>/<i>/картинка.png` (sRGB 8 bit of the region — to look at), `журнал.jsonl` (the lines that will go into the journal), `вариант.json` (the piece's response, words, `однообразие` — an estimate, the number of journal lines, the line «how to take»), `вариант.pkl` (the fields of the layers only of the region, compressed; the whole canvas is not stored); into `<в_папку>/` — **`рядом.png`**: «холст сейчас» and all the variants as one sheet 1:1 with the caption «number: response», and `варианты.json` (n, the region, the fingerprint of the canvas). The former variants of this folder with a number ≥ n are removed. In another launch: `х = Холст.загрузить('лист.npz', журнал='лист.jsonl')`, `х.взять('варианты/фигура/1')` — the pixels, the journal and `.npz` are the same bytes that `кусок(х, 1)` directly in this canvas would give; on cores — as on one; a replay of the journal — the same `.npz`. To look at a variant without laying it — `кисть.варианты.прочесть('варианты/фигура/1')`: a `Вариант` with `картинка`, `ответ`, `слова`, `однообразие`. **Someone else's folder of variants** (sent, downloaded, from an archive, of another model) is taken in the same way and without risk: `вариант.pkl` is read without executing code from it — in the file there is only the engine's data (numbers, strings, arrays, dictionaries, brushes, paints, stroke responses); if there is anything else — `ValueError` with the name, the canvas and the journal are not touched. If the piece returned an object of its own class (not a number, a string, an array, a dictionary, a stroke response) — the same error on your own folder too: then `х.взять(путь, своя=True)` / `прочесть(путь, своя=True)` — an ordinary pickle, it executes what is named in the file, therefore only for a folder you computed yourself; simpler — to return a dictionary from the piece. Three rules: (1) **before `варианты(…, в_папку=)` — `х.сохранить(…)`**, and take — into a canvas loaded from this `.npz` with this journal, before new strokes and labels: the fingerprint of the canvas (size, ground, grain, seed, stroke counter, stage and object of the label, repeats of seeds, fields of the layers in the variant's region and whether there is wet outside it) is checked — if it is not that canvas or the journal is not at that place — `ValueError` with what differed; (2) **the piece creates or charges the brushes itself** (`к = Кисть.из_файла(…)` or `к.вытереть()` and a color in the piece): the brushes of another launch are not touched by the variant, and the dirt and paint that were on the brushes of script 1 before the piece do not travel into script 2 — in the variant's journal they are as their own lines, the replay is right, but `кусок(х, i)` directly in script 2 with clean brushes would give something else; (3) the journal of a variant on disk names the brushes of the piece anew (lines `def`), as after `загрузить`, — `х.взять(вв[i])` in the same launch lays the same lines, without `в_папку` everything is as before. If the canvas was loaded without `журнал=` — the warning «строк варианта в журнале не будет». It costs over and above the variants: on 400×300 — fractions of a second and ~1.8 MB per variant of a region 270×220; on a canvas of 2400×1600 in 27 layers, region 700×700, 3 variants of 12 strokes each on 3 cores — `варианты` with `в_папку` 5.9 s (without — 1.2 s), 38 MB per variant (the fields of all layers of the region), `х.взять('<папка>/<i>')` 0.8–0.9 s (the piece directly — 0.3 s): only the region is checked, the fields outside it are not read.

```python
import os, tempfile
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, путь_с_нажимом
папка = tempfile.mkdtemp()
лист, журнал = os.path.join(папка, 'лист.npz'), os.path.join(папка, 'лист.jsonl')

def кусок(у, i):                                   # кисть — своя в куске: в другом запуске она та же
    к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=14.0)
    у.пометка('проба', этап=1, объект=f'проба {i}')
    for j in range(4):
        у.мазок(путь_с_нажимом([(150 + 40 * j, 160), (175 + 40 * j - 20 * i, 210)], 14.0, нажим=0.8), (70 + 60 * i, 110, 170), к, слой='проба')
    return {'i': i}

# --- скрипт 1: сохранить, варианты на диск, закончиться
х = Холст(500, 400, px_на_мм=8.0, журнал=журнал, ядра=2)
х.мазок(путь_с_нажимом([(20, 200), (480, 210)], 30.0, нажим=0.7), (160, 140, 120), Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=30.0))
х.сохранить(лист)
х.варианты(кусок, 3, (100, 100, 400, 280), в_папку=os.path.join(папка, 'варианты'))
х.закрыть()                                          # глазами — варианты/рядом.png, варианты/<i>/картинка.png

# --- скрипт 2 (другой запуск Python): тот же .npz с тем же журналом, выбранный — в холст
х = Холст.загрузить(лист, журнал=журнал)
х.взять(os.path.join(папка, 'варианты', '2'))       # то же, что кусок(х, 2) прямо в этот холст
х.сохранить(лист)
```
- Rectangle of a stroke: `п = х.прямоугольник_мазка(точки, к, сид=…, цвет=…, слой=…, нагрузка=…, …)` — with the same arguments that will go into `мазок` (without them or with `точно=True` — a stroke with the default arguments). This is the frame of the stroke itself, outside which it neither reads nor writes the canvas: it is computed by the stroke's own computing (the path, the strands, their lag and drift to the side) up to the place where the stroke takes the canvas, — 7–14 % of the time of the stroke, it does not touch pixels; a stroke with these arguments will not go outside it. It is wider than the trace of the strands by the stroke's margins — 8 px and the bank of wet paint (2 px + 0.15 of the brush width); for a soft brush — also the place of its hairs on the axis of the sweep. `None` — the stroke will not touch the canvas. A stroke whose rectangle is inside a subarea gives on the subarea the same pixels as on the canvas. A stroke with arguments other than those asked about will lie differently — ask for its frame with them.
- Remove the wet: `х.снять(точки, 'тряпка', ширина=40, сид=…, слой=None)` or `'скребок'` (journal — `"tool"`). **Scratch out** — `о = х.снять(точки, 'черенок', ширина=8, слой=None)`: the end of the brush handle, a hard hemisphere with a diameter of `ширина` px (1–3 mm — 8–24 px at 8 px/mm); the points — `(x, y, нажим)`, it does not take a seed. It does not remove paint but pushes it apart: a furrow, at the sides — beads, at the lift — a lump; the volume of paint is the same (only a bead that went past the edge of the canvas is lost). **Pressure is depth:** from 0.8 the end goes over what is hard — along the axis the dry layer is open (on the ground a tone remains, as under the scraper); weaker — there is a film under the end: mm = 0.04 × the diameter of the end in mm × (0.8 / pressure − 1) (end 1 mm: pressure 0.6 — 0.013 mm, what is lower shows through; 0.3 — 0.07 mm, a furrow in the paint; 3 mm — three times thicker). The end is round: the clean band along the axis is 2–4 px, toward the sides the film is thicker, the furrow at the surface — up to the diameter of the end; the thinner the dark layer, the sharper the line. Hard is what is dry, what has set, the grain of the ground, with `слой=` also the paint of other layers: the end goes over its tops, in the pits (grain, furrows of a dry layer) paint remains. The response `Процарапано` (`print(о)`): `длина` px; `до_дна` — the share of the path with an open bottom; `осталось` — the film under the axis, mm; `на_твёрдом` — the share of the path with pressure from 0.8; `ширина` of the furrow, px; `глубже`, mm; `сдвинуто` (h·px²), `мм3`; if empty — `почему`. Example — `примеры/черенок.py`.
- **Correction of what has dried** — correction of a place, not of the sheet. `х.размочить(доля=1.0, слой=None, область=None)` — the counterpart of `подсушить`: oil over the dry, the top of the dried paint is wet again, and blending (`нагрузка=0`), rag, scraper and knife take this place as wet. What is re-wetted is the outer skin of the film — no deeper than 0.25 h (0.1 mm) from the surface: thin painting (film up to 0.25) comes alive entirely, in an impasto bank the body under the outer skin remains hard (a knife without paint will remove from it only the outer skin — up to 0.2 h from the tops); a repeated call does not go deeper, a layer above takes away depth by its own thickness; the degree of set (`схватить`) is removed in the same measure. `доля=0` — nothing. The response is the area where moisture grew, px. Journal — `{"wet": …}`. What has been re-wetted mixes anew with what is laid on top — wet a place (`область=`), and not the sheet.
- `о = х.прижать(точки, кисть=None, ширина=None, слой=None, глубина=None, этап=None, объект=None, намерение=None)` — a sweep of the palette knife laid flat over dried paint: it cuts off what is above the plane of the knife — the tops of a dry bank, the ridges of impasto, a hump. `точки` — as for a stroke (`жест`, `путь_с_нажимом`, your own `(x, y, нажим)`); the knife is `кисть` of the form `мастихин` (the width is taken) or `ширина` of the blade edge in px, one of the two. Depth is driven by pressure: up to 0.3 the knife rides over the tops and takes nothing, 0.6 — halfway to the body, from 0.9 the blade lands on the body of the paint under it — the median of the heights of the paint under the sole 4 mm × blade edge — and does not go lower: the ridges of impasto are cut off to its middle (the hollows remain). The support of the blade is the paint, and not the ground beside it: a point from a quarter of the highest under the sole holds in full measure, a thinner one — in the measure of its thickness, bare ground does not hold; where the blade edge comes off the bank obliquely and for the most part hangs over the ground, the blade stands on the bank and does not cut it down to the ground (a sweep at any angle to the bank). A bank on an even thin layer is cut down to the layer, a bank on bare ground — to its own middle. `глубина` — mm, a cautious sweep: the blade does not land lower than the highest point under the knife by more than this much per sweep (`None` — without a limit). What is cut off (dry and wet) goes onto the knife and off the canvas — the thickness grows nowhere, there is no bead; the color of the layer stays in place; where the layer is cut through — the lower one is open; the ground, the grain and the layers not named in `слой` are not cut (`None` — all, from top to bottom); a named layer is cut only where there is no paint of another layer above it, — under a whole upper one the knife does not reach it. Outside the knife's sweep the canvas is bit for bit. The response is `Прижато`: `срезано` (px with a cut), `под_ножом` (px), `на_нож` (volume, h·px²) and `мм3`, `доля` (what is cut off to the paint under the knife), `глубже` (the deepest cut, mm), `сырого` (the share of wet in what is cut off), `прямоугольник`; if nothing is cut off — `bool(о)` is false and `о.почему` as a word. A clean canvas, an impasto sweep of bristle 60 px (`нагрузка=1.0`), a 44 px knife drawn across it, pressure 1: the ridges (p99 − median of the relief) per sweep × 0.38–0.78 (12 seeds), per two × 0.15–0.66, on average × 0.4 (48 seeds); an oblique sweep through the same bank at 0–60° — down to the ground 0 px. A brush of brush hair still does not dent the dry. Journal — a line with `"tool": "нож"`. The cut is smooth, without the texture of a stroke: if more than 10 % of the canvas is cut off — the hint «срезано ножом». Example — `примеры/правка.py`, test — `тесты/test_правка_М3.py`.
- Output: `х.png(путь, свет=True, сила=None, тень=0)` (PNG RGB 16 bit), `х.png_слоя(путь, слой)` (RGBA), `х.png_толщины(путь, слой=None, легенда=True)` (gray 16 bit, the scale is absolute, one for all canvases: 0 — no paint, 65535 — 1.6 mm and thicker, mm per level — the chunk tEXt `kist:mm_per_level`; beside it — `<имя>.легенда.png` 8 bit: the same map (gray is the root of the share of the scale, thin 0.05–0.2 mm is distinguishable), a band of the scale with captions in mm, the greatest thickness and the share of the place thicker than the scale; the response is the path of the legend; `легенда=False` — only 16 bit), `х.png_высот(путь, без=None, до=None, полный=False)` (a height map for printing: gray 16 bit pixel for pixel with `х.png`, the height of the paint above the ground in mm — 0.001 mm per level, up to 65.5 mm; the height is as for the light: a layer above floods the furrows of the lower ones, `полный=True` — a simple sum of the layer thicknesses; the grain of the ground is not included; `без`, `до` — as in `взгляд`; mm per level and px/mm — in tEXt chunks and in `<имя>.json` beside it: `мм_на_уровень`, `px_на_мм`, the size in px and mm, `наибольшая_высота_мм`, the layers; the response is the same dictionary), `х.png_света(путь, сила=None, тень=0)`, `х.картинка(свет, сила, область, тень=0, поверхность=None)` — numpy (H, W, 3) float32, linear light 0..1; into sRGB — `кисть.краска.из_линейного`; `поверхность` — «Light over the relief».
- State: `х.сохранить('лист.npz')`, `Холст.загрузить('лист.npz')`; with a journal — `Холст.загрузить('этап4.npz', журнал='лист.jsonl')`: the journal is trimmed to the line `{"saved": "этап4.npz"}` and is written further; the lines after it (if there are any) — into `лист.jsonl.до_отката` byte for byte (if it exists — `.до_отката.2`, `.3`, …: the former copy is not overwritten), in stderr — «журнал обрезан до этап4.npz: N строк после неё — в лист.jsonl.до_отката»; to bring back what was erased — join the file and the copy (`cat лист.jsonl лист.jsonl.до_отката`); if there is no tail — there is neither copy nor line. **Without `журнал=`** the canvas takes the journal by which it was written **only for reading**: the path of the journal lies beside it as a file `<имя>.npz.путь_журнала` (full and relative to `.npz` — the folder may be moved; `.npz` itself is the former one byte for byte), and `х.однообразие()`, `х.регулярность()`, `х.виды`, `х.манера` read it up to the line of this save (they do not see the strokes of further stages); `х.журнал_путь` is this path (it can also be given to a replay), the journal itself is not written: to write — only with `журнал=`. If there are strokes after such a loading — a gauge by the journal gives an error in words (the journal does not know them); if there is no journal file or `.npz` has no path file beside it (a former one, from a replay) — the error «у холста нет журнала» with what is missing.
- Layers: `х.слои` — from bottom to top, read only (`х.слои.pop(…)` — an error with a hint). Remove a layer — `х.убрать_слой('середина')`, rearrange — `х.порядок_слоёв(['низ', 'середина', 'верх'])` (all the names once each); both edits go into the journal. To look without a layer, changing nothing, — `х.взгляд(…, без=['середина'])` or `до='середина'` (only the layers below), likewise `х.виды`, `х.обзор` («Eyes»).
- The seed of a stroke without its own `сид=` is taken from the seed of the sheet (`Холст(…, сид=0)`), the stage and the object (`х.пометка(…, этап=, объект=)`) and the points of the stroke themselves — not from its number: a stroke inserted or removed in another place does not touch the seeds of others. A stroke with the same points in the same object takes a different seed the second time. Which seed a stroke will take — `х.сид_мазка(точки)`.
- Light over the relief — the default strength is 0.40; a thin sweep as its own layer over thick paint is lit along the rim only to the extent of its own thickness. The light follows the thickness: the share of light (σ L with light minus σ without) for a fill with `плоская` 30 with load 0.1 / 0.3 / `'обычная'` / 0.6 / 1 / `'пастозная'` — 1 / 3 / 3 / 7 / 14 / 11 L: a thin film hardly catches it, the ripple of a thin fill is the tone of the paint (`разброс_тона`), and not the light; there is no need to weaken the strength of the light on the whole sheet. White paint and light paint do not go gray under the light: light paint returns, by a share of its albedo, the light that has left the slopes, and the light above white overflows into neighboring pixels where there is room up to white (1 mm) — thick white paint over white under light 0.4 is darker than itself without light by 1 L (jabs in three layers — by 1 L, individual ones), dark paint holds the shadow of the furrows as it was.
- Surface — an output key `х.картинка(…, поверхность=)` and `х.взгляд(…, поверхность=)`: `'матовое'` (dry: a whitish haze of 3.5 %, the highlight is wide and weak — gypsum, tempera), `'полуматовое'` (oil: a highlight along the ridges of thick paint, the color is a little deeper), `'лак'` (the highlight is sharper and brighter, the color is deeper — the dark is darker, more saturated); `None` (default) — a weak narrow highlight without haze: by eye it is dry, like `'матовое'`; oil — `'полуматовое'`, gloss — `'лак'`. A thin film hardly catches the highlight, a thick one — on the ridges. Output only: the canvas, layers and journal do not change (it does not go into the journal; `png` and a replay do not take the key), without light the key does nothing; another word — `ValueError` with a list.
- Cast shadow — `тень=True` (0.6) or 0–1: a high edge of thick paint darkens what is behind it away from the light (the light is from the upper left). Without `тень` there is none.
- Grain of the canvas — `Холст(…, px_на_мм=8.0, зерно=(0.6, 1.0))`: the thread spacing in mm and the depth. Without it the ground is smooth.

### Retroactive edits

If toward the end of the sheet you saw that the bottom is cool or the mix of the sky is the wrong one — edit the journal lines with one command, and not by painting over (an extra layer, murk) and not with your own code over the jsonl. The edit is by a filter, only what depends on the edit is rebuilt; the result is bit for bit the same as a full replay of the edited journal.

- **Live canvas:** `о = х.править(фильтр…, правка…, путь='до_после.png')` — for a canvas with `журнал=`; after the call the canvas is already rebuilt, the brushes are the same objects, on them is what a full replay would give; you paint on with the same canvas. Snapshots taken before the edit lapse. A canvas from `Холст.загрузить(…, журнал=)` does not know the frames of the strokes before the loading — an edit of those lines goes by an honest replay of the whole journal (said in the response).
- **Journal file:** `python -m кисть.задним лист.jsonl --out лист.png …` (filter, edit, `--before-after до_после.png`); from Python — `кисть.задним.править('лист.jsonl', out='лист.png', …)`. The checkpoints of the former replay are needed (`<out>.точки/`, written by `python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --out лист.png`): the checkpoint of the end and the frames of the strokes; the nearest checkpoint before the first edited line is the base. Without them — an honest replay from the nearest checkpoint (`--resume`), said in the response.
- **Filter** (all together — «and»): `строки_с='4-200'` (or `(4, 200)`, one line — a number; `--lines`), `слой=` (`--layer`), `этап=`, `объект=` (if the stroke has no fields of its own — from the label above; `--stage`, `--object`), `замес=` (the name of the mix, `--swatch`), `область=(x0, y0, x1, y1)` — a point of the stroke's path is inside, px of the journal (`--area`).
- **Edit:** `удалить=True` (`--delete`; the line becomes a note `note`, the former line — in its field `deleted`, the line numbers and the seeds of the other strokes are the same); `цвет=(r, g, b)` (`--color`); `новый_замес=имя` (`--set-swatch`); a shift of tone in OKLab — `dL=`, `dC=`, `dh=` (degrees), `теплее=` (+b; for a stroke by a mix — from the color of the mix, the stroke leaves the mix); `сдвиг=(dx, dy)` px (`--move`); `нагрузка=` (`--load`); `нажим=` (for a sweep with anchor points) and `нажим_в=` — a multiplier (`--pressure`, `--pressure-mul`); `ширина=` (`--size`; for a brush of the journal of a live canvas the width is in its line `"def"`, it is not changed by an edit). For a stroke whose color is the charged paint of the brush, the tone is not changed: an error with the line number.
- **The former journal** — beside it, `<журнал>.до_правки` (the second edit — `.до_правки.2`, …); at the end of the journal — a note `note` «правка задним числом» with the filter and the edit (the field `edit`). If not a single stroke fell under the filter — an error, the journal is not touched.
- **Response** (`print(о)` — as a line): `о['правлено']` lines, `о['пересчитано']` strokes out of `о['всего']` and `о['доля']`, `о['площадь']` — the share of the sheet under what is affected, `о['время']` s, `о['как']` — `'затронутое'` or `'честный повтор: почему'`, `о['рамка']` of what is affected, `о['путь']` — a PNG «до | после» by the frame of what is affected 1:1, `о['запас']` — the former journal.
- **What is affected:** the edited strokes and everything later that depends on them along the chain: the frame of a stroke crosses the frame of what is affected (for an edited one — both the former and the new), a brush carries what was picked up by an affected stroke (without a wiping between them), a drying by an edited line — its region. For what is affected to lie as in a full replay, the strokes before it in the same place and with the same brush are laid anew too; the others are only taken into account (seed, counter, layer) and are not computed. Outside what is affected — the same bytes.
- **Price:** an edit of a common bottom (the underpainting of the whole sky, all the strokes of a layer) affects almost the whole sheet — a recomputing of almost everything, this is an honest price, and not a malfunction; an edit of one place or of several lines — fractions of the sheet. If from 90 % of the strokes after the base are affected — the edit itself goes by an honest replay from the base: it is cheaper than laying almost everything and splicing (said in the response). Tape, knife, removal, `убрать_слой`, `порядок_слоёв`, an edit of a label or of the canvas in the edited piece — an honest replay: their dependence is not by frames.

Example — the bottom of 30 sweeps, the top of 60 over it, the bottom of one cell warmer by 0.02 (`примеры/задним.py`, test `тесты/test_задним_К3.py`):

```python
import os, tempfile
from примеры.задним import низ_и_верх, МЕСТО
папка = tempfile.mkdtemp()
х, низ, верх = низ_и_верх(os.path.join(папка, 'лист.jsonl'))
о = х.править(слой='низ', область=МЕСТО, теплее=0.02, путь=os.path.join(папка, 'до_после.png'))
print(о)      # правка: строк правлено 1, пересчитано 3 из 90 мазков (3,3 %), площадь 2,8 % листа, …; затронутое
```

### Tape stencil

A shape with a clean edge (a logo, a letter, a window, the edge of a house) — with tape: tape over what is around the shape, go with sweeps across the edge, remove. Your own code for trimming sweeps by a mask is not needed: the edge comes from the paint, and not from an outline.

- `л = х.скотч(маска, слой=None, имя=None, высота_мм=0.1)` — a masking tape where `маска` (bool, `(высота, ширина)` of the canvas) is true; the response is `Лента` (`л.имя`, `л.маска`, `л.слой`, `л.высота_мм`). The paint of strokes over the tape lies on the tape, and not on the canvas and not on the layer under it. `слой=None` — the tape is on all layers; `слой='имя'` — only for strokes into this layer, other layers do not see it. `имя` — for `снять_скотч(имя)`; without it — «лента 1», «лента 2»… Several tapes at once are possible. While the tape is lying, `картинка` and `взгляд` show under it the color of the ground and the paint on the tape.
- `х.снять_скотч(имя=None)` — the tape goes away together with the paint on it: under it — what was there before it, bit for bit; past the tape edge — the paint as it lay. Without a name all are removed, starting from the top one. A tape on which another is lying — `ValueError` «сперва её». The response is `{имя: {'затёк': px, 'разрыв': px}}`: the area of the seepage under the tape edge and the place at the tape edge where wet impasto went away with the tape. `х.ленты` — the tapes that are lying now.
- **At the tape edge — physics, and not a shape.** The tape edge is a step of its height. A brush going onto the tape rakes paint against the wall: at the tape edge on the canvas a bead ~0.2 mm wide remains, the higher, the thicker the paint and the stronger the pressure. A hair coming off the tape does not touch the canvas at once: the slit is ~0.15 mm at pressure 0.6 and shorter at a strong one; thick paint and flowing paint (`разбавитель`, `ровно`) close over the slit. Flowing paint that lay at the tape edge seeps under the tape up to 0.2 mm (deeper at strong pressure); thick paint and dry paint do not seep. Wet impasto that lay across the tape edge as one film breaks at removal: a part of the body at the tape edge, up to 0.25 mm, goes away with the tape. The dry (`подсушить` before removal) is cut evenly. The tape edge is a line, and not a staircase of mask pixels: a mask pixel on the staircase is only partly under the tape and carries paint by a share.
- **Numbers of the test bench** (`проверка/замер_скотча.py`, 12 seeds; a star of 10 rays, `плоская` 44, 10 sweeps across the rays, pressure 0.6 and 0.85, load 0.5 and `'пастозная'`, 8 px/mm). Under the tape farther than 2 px from the tape edge there is no paint (0 %). With thick paint in the second row under the tape 0 %, with flowing paint (thinner 0.5) — 39 % of the pixels, coverage 0.11; the first row with thick paint — 52 % of the pixels with coverage 0.12 (this is the share of a pixel of the tape edge, not seepage). Inside the shape at 2 px from the tape edge the coverage is not lower than 0.935 with thick paint and 0.918 with flowing paint (without the tape — 0.971 and 0.928). The sublayer under the tape is bit for bit.
- While the tape is lying, strokes are computed in this process, without cores (a sweep writes to the tape the seepage at its edge). In `варианты` with processes the bead and the slit go into the canvas, but the seepage from the sweeps of the piece is not remembered by the tape: sweeps at the tape edge — in the canvas itself. On a subarea (`х.участок`) a tape cannot be laid.
- Journal: `{"tape": имя, "shape": [в, ш], "mask": …}` (`"layer"`, `"height"` in mm) and `{"untape": имя или null}`, a replay — bit for bit. `х.сохранить` and `Холст.загрузить` hold the tapes (the mask, what is under it, the seepage); they can be removed after loading. A canvas without a tape is saved and laid with the same bytes as before tape.

```python
import numpy as np
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, жест
х = Холст(600, 600, грунт=(40, 38, 42), px_на_мм=8)
yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:600, 0:600] + 0.5
уг, д = np.arctan2(yy - 300, xx - 300), np.hypot(yy - 300, xx - 300)
звезда = д < 105 + 145 * np.abs((уг / (np.pi / 10)) % 2 - 1)      # 10 лучей: вершины на 250 px, впадины на 105
х.скотч(~звезда)                                                    # лента вокруг звезды
к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=44)
for i in range(10):                                                  # широкие ходы через лучи, края не обводить
    а, н = i * np.pi / 5, i * np.pi / 5 + 1.2                        # луч и ход под углом к нему
    cx, cy = 300 + 190 * np.cos(а), 300 + 190 * np.sin(а)
    х.мазок(жест([(cx - 140 * np.cos(н), cy - 140 * np.sin(н)), (cx + 140 * np.cos(н), cy + 140 * np.sin(н))], 44, 'отрыв',
                 сид=i, нажим=0.85), (230, 190, 60), к, нагрузка='пастозная')
print(х.снять_скотч())                                              # {'лента 1': {'затёк': 0, 'разрыв': …}}
х.взгляд((440, 230, 540, 330), 4, путь='кромка_x4.png')            # кромка луча ×4: край по маске, без светлого контура
```

### Other public

- `х.досчитать()` — finish computing the strokes that are still being computed on cores (the canvas, layers and brushes do this themselves before any reading); `х.закрыть()` — finish computing, stop the computing processes and close the journal. `Холст.загрузить(путь, …, ядра=None)` — `ядра` as for `Холст(…, ядра=)`. `Слой(высота, ширина, общая=False)` — `общая=True`: arrays in the shared memory of the computing processes (a canvas on cores creates its layers this way itself).
- **Hints along the way** — `Холст(…, подсказки=None)`, `Холст.загрузить(…, подсказки=None)`: None — by the environment `KIST_HINTS` (`0` — none), False — none. At a boundary — a change of stage or object by a label, `х.сохранить`, `х.закрыть`, the end of the process — the engine itself writes the line `подсказка: <факт числами> — под рукой: <вызов>, <вызов>…` to stderr (the warning `кисть.Подсказка`, like `ПустойМазок`) and the same one to stdout (if stdout and stderr are not one file: in a terminal there is one line; `2>/dev/null` and `grep` on stderr do not hide it; at the end of the process — only stderr, so as not to stand after the last line of the script, and in the field of the response it will come in the next process) about what is visible from the count of strokes and the journal: **серия** — ≥ 40 sweeps of an object (without a label — of a layer) of one brush, width (in steps of twofold), slant sector of 30° and load (in steps of 0.1), a wiped brush does not count; **пуск этапа** — a stage is written from the same save (a new journal — with the same first label) for the 3rd time; **глаза** — ≥ 1000 strokes without `взгляд`, `виды`, `обзор`, while the canvas was output whole (`png`, `картинка` without a region), once per stage; `картинка` and `png` whole do not reset the count (the hint sounds on the output itself), and the line says so: «N мазков без взгляд, виды, обзор (png, картинка — не в счёт); выведен целиком K раз»; **масштаб** — a canvas that is not 8 px/mm and the first stroke with a brush that holds px («Scale» in «Concepts»): once per canvas, on this stroke — «холст 4 px/мм, физика на 8: px держат узкая уже 6 мм (24 px; при 8 px/мм — уже 3 мм), мягкая, сухая, кромка густой; «к» 2 мм = 8 px: узкая»; at 8 px/mm and without `px_на_мм` it is silent; **ответы** — ≥ 10 strokes of an object without a trace, while the stroke response was read for fewer than 1 % of the sweeps; **первый мазок слоя** — `закрыто` < 0.1 (if «серый мягкой» has not already told of it); **сырое чужое** and **серый мягкой** — on the stroke itself (on cores — at a boundary), by `о.чужое` and `о.серый` of the response («Stroke response» — thresholds): «ход по сырому чужому цвету: подобрано 1,30, тон сдвинут на ΔL -119 / Δh +167° (лежало L 60, названо L 220)», at hand `смешение_для`, `х.подсушить`, a layer of its own; «мягкая по сухому в новом слое — серый: закрыто 0,19, тон следа L 118 — 37 % пути от лежащего…», at hand `нагрузка_для`, `х.подсушить`, `х.проба`; each — once per stage (without a stage — per object, without that — per layer), beyond three lines per process it waits in `х.подсказки`, while `о.чужое` and `print(о)` tell for each sweep; a replay of the journal does not make the measurement and is silent; **по одному месту** — the soft brush with paint ≥ 20 times over the wet of its own layer (`о.сырое['по сырому']` ≥ 0.5) through one tile of 64 px; **разброс цвета** — ≥ 40 sweeps of an object with paint, and the named colors of neighboring sweeps (the center of the sweep in one tile of 16 mm) diverged in a, b OKLab by no more than σ 0.010 and in L by no more than σ 4 — the count is by the named colors (the argument `цвет` or the set), not by pixels; if `х.манера(…, образец=)` was called in the sheet, the line also has its «цвет в пятне» of the canvas and the reference; **толстый слой** — a layer, where lower ones lie under it, is by the median 4 times or more thicker than their sum (the lower ones lie beneath a quarter of its place and thicker than 0.01 mm): impasto over a rub ×4.5–6.2, ordinary over a rub and impasto with `разбавитель=1` ×2.4–2.5 are silent; **валы по границам** — the ridges of the relief of a layer (higher than both neighbors at 0.75 and 1.5 mm across by 0.3 of its mean thickness, on average over a cell of ~1 mm) in a band of ±2 mm at the borders of tone (a drop of the squinted L ≥ 24 over 4 mm) occupy ≥ 5 % of the place and twice as much as in the body — a sign of outlines; a layer covered by the layers above by more than 3/4 is not counted; **пастозно** — for an object (without labels — for a layer) its layers are thicker than 0.3 mm on ≥ 25 % of the area (in cells of 32 px under its sweeps); in the line — how many of the sweeps of this brush in the object and the tone of the trace against the named one (ΔL, Δa, b); `к.вытереть()` clears the memory of the brush, there is no automatic wiping; мест от 0,6 — 14 из 14»; about a series of which **серия** has already told, it does not sound with a line of its own — the address comes in the field `подсказки` and in `х.подсказки`; a canvas without a journal is silent; the journal is read on as it grows (a sheet of ~1200 sweeps, 24 boundaries — 0.2 % of the time); example — `тесты/test_зеркало_адрес.py`. This is a fact, not an order: what to do, the line does not say, the order of the calls in it is rearranged. Each — once per sheet (the memory is the file `<журнал>.подсказки.json`; `загрузить(…, журнал=)` takes the count as of that save; if the file is erased — from the beginning), per boundary — one line (the others wait: «(ещё N: х.подсказки)»), per process — three. In stderr — one line of its own, without a source line under it. **The field `подсказки` of the response**: `в = х.взгляд(…)` — the same uint8 array and `в.подсказки`; `о = х.сохранить(путь)` — `о.путь`, `о.счётчик` (of strokes), `о.подсказки`: the texts of the hints, both told and waiting ones, that have not yet been in this field (each — once per sheet, the limit of three lines per process does not hold it back); if there are no new ones or the hints are turned off — empty. **Restart of a stage:** `загрузить(…, журнал=)` and a new journal from the beginning check the memory against the count of strokes — what was told and what is waiting, counted by strokes that are no longer in the journal (after this save), is forgotten and does not sound (the same pair later does not sound again); at a boundary — the same check. `print(х.подсказки)` — what was told and what is waiting; `х.подсказки.выключить()` or one kind — `х.подсказки.выключить('серия')` (`'пуск этапа'`, `'глаза'`, `'ответы'`, `'первый мазок слоя'`, `'по одному месту'`, `'разброс цвета'`, `'толстый слой'`, `'валы по границам'`, `'пастозно'`, `'грязь на кисти'`, `'однообразие места'`, `'масштаб'`, `'цвет формулой'`, `'раскраска точками'`, `'срезано ножом'`, `'сырое чужое'`, `'серый мягкой'`; the file of the sheet remembers what is turned off); `warnings.filterwarnings('ignore', category=кисть.Подсказка)` in a script mutes only stderr (Python rejects the option `-W ignore::кисть.Подсказка`: «invalid module name» — the module name is not in Latin letters). The import of `кисть` replaces `warnings.formatwarning` for the whole process: a hint is printed as one line of its own, other warnings — as they were. An example of the channel (`2>/dev/null`, the field of the response, a stage from a save) — `тесты/test_подсказки_канал.py`. Always silent: `кисть.повтор`, `х.проба`, the subareas of `варианты` and `перебор`. Pixels, seeds and the journal with hints and without are the same bytes.
- **Hint «под верхним слоем»** — on the stroke itself (on cores — at a boundary), once per stage (without a stage — per object, without that — per layer): a sweep lay under the paint of the layers above and is visible on the sheet by less than 0.25 of the trace (`о.видно`, `о.закрыл`): «ход в слой «низ»: видно на листе 0,00 — закрыл слой «верх»»; at hand — `х.порядок_слоёв(имена)`, a layer of its own on top (`слой=` in `х.мазок`), `х.взгляд(область, без=['верх'])`. To turn off — `х.подсказки.выключить('под верхним слоем')`. A canvas with `замер=False` is silent. **Stage summary** (Sv-1) — `х.сводка(этап=None)`: the facts of a stage as numbers, no more than 12 lines. It is printed to stdout by itself: on `х.сохранить` — about the current stage, on an `х.пометка(…, этап=)` that changed the stage — about the one that ended; if there are no new strokes since the last printing or the stage is empty — it is silent. The same — as the field `.сводка` of the response of `х.сохранить` and as the file `<путь>.сводка.txt` beside the save (if the stage is empty — there is no file). If no stage was set — about all strokes without a stage; if the stage is given on the strokes themselves — about the stage of the last one. Lines: how many strokes and how many responses read (at least one field or `print(о)`); ragged — the sweep broke (`о.разрывы`), a ragged edge (`о.край['слово']`); how many traces with `о.просветы` from 0.10 and the worst by a frame; the tone of the trace from the named one — for how many ΔL is from 8 or Δa, b from 0.02 (sweeps with paint, without the invisible ones), the farthest of all — by a frame; the invisible ones — `о.видно` less than 0.25 on their own stroke and by which layer they are covered; without a trace — by causes; the most monotonous place of the stage by a frame, by the journal (from 30 sweeps in a cell of 50 mm; the score is as for `х.однообразие()`; without a journal there is no such line); the hints of the whole sheet after which not one call «под рукой» was called (what is visible is the calls of the canvas, the reading of the response, `нагрузка_для`, `смешение_для`, the gauge; a replay from the command line is not visible, the place of the call is not checked). The numbers as a dictionary — `.числа`: `с = х.сводка(); с.числа['невидимые']`. The count is as of the moment of the stroke: what lay on top later does not go into «невидимые»; `х.откатить` and `Холст.загрузить(…, журнал=)` restore the count, a retroactive edit does not recompute it. It is silent together with the hints (`подсказки=False`, the environment `KIST_HINTS`, `х.подсказки.выключить()`); `кисть.повтор`, `х.проба`, subareas — always. It does not affect the pixels and the journal; the cost is about 1.5 ms per stroke. «Порван ход» also counts a sweep whose paint ran out before its end (like `о.разрывы`). The calls that the engine makes itself inside `х.проба`, `х.варианты` and the like (`снимок`, `откатить`) also go into «called»: a hint may leave the line before it has been answered. `.числа['место']` — a dictionary (`рамка`, `мазков`, `оценка`, `узко`) or `None`, `.числа['неотвеченные']` — the codes of the kinds (in the line — their names).
- `Холст.слои[имя]` — `Слой`: arrays (H, W) float32 — `цвет` (H, W, 3, linear light), `покрытие` 0–1, `толщина` (units of h), `влажность` 0–1 (1 — wet), `схват` 0–1, `пропуск` (how much of the lower one is visible through paint that is not opaque; `None` until there is any), `текучая` (the trace of a flowing brush `ровно`; `None` until there is any). Read for measurements; write — only by the calls of the canvas.
- `х.рельеф(y0=0, y1=None, x0=0, x1=None, полный=False)` — the thickness of the paint of all layers; the upper layer floods the relief of the lower ones (by 70 % at full coverage), `полный=True` — a simple sum.
- `х.сохранить(путь, сверх=None)`, `Холст.загрузить(путь, сверх=False, журнал=None)` — `сверх`: (a JSON dictionary, arrays) of your own to put into the same `.npz`; on loading `сверх=True` — the response `(холст, словарь, массивы)`.
- `х.заметка(что, этап=None, объект=None)` — `что`: any JSON. `х.порядок_слоёв(имена)` — `имена`: all layers from bottom to top. `х.сид_мазка(точки, этап=None, объект=None, взять=False)` — `взять=True` counts the stroke in (`мазок` does so; you need not do it yourself).
- `тычок(x, y, нажим=0.85, угол=None, мс=30.0)` — `мс`: how long the brush stands; if not 30 — the journal writes `"core"`.
- `повторить(строки, ширина=4800, высота=3000, грунт=…, масштаб=1.0, пресет=None, сид=0, печать=None, состояние=None, начало=1, отбор=None, точка=None)` — `печать`: the function by which to tell the progress (once per 1000 strokes); `отбор`: `(этап, объект)`, `None` — any.
- Brush: `Кисть.из_словаря(д)` — from a dictionary of fields (like `"def"`), `к.в_файл(путь)` — into a preset .json, `к.в_пикселях(px_на_мм)` — a copy with the width in px (for a brush in mm), `к.грязная()` — whether there is foreign paint on the tips, `к.толщина_плёнки(нагрузка=None)` — the film thickness of a full stroke (h), `к.набрана(нагрузка=None)` — the load as a number. The fields `форма` (`'плоская'`, `'круглая'`, `'мастихин'`), `толщина` (the multiplier of the film thickness), `разброс_жёсткости` (the spread of the pressure of the tips: the depth of the furrows), `бродяги` (has no effect, left for old presets).
- `кисть.НАГРУЗКИ` — a load by name as a number; `кисть.ВИДЫ` — the turbidity of a kind of paint by name. `смешать(a, b, доля_b=0.5)` — a mixture of two paints in linear RGB (`доля_b` — a number or an array), `смешать_srgb(a, b, доля_b=0.5)` — the same in sRGB 0..255.

## Eyes

A look at and a measurement of the canvas in one call. The canvas is only read: the pixels, seeds and journal do not change. **Lightness L** everywhere is OKLab lightness expressed in the sRGB gray scale 0–255 (the gray (v, v, v) — L = v); a, b — the OKLab color plane. The **target** (reference) compared with: a picture — a path to a PNG/JPG or an array (H, W, 3) sRGB 0–255 in canvas coordinates; a canvas — a `Холст` or a path to the `.npz` of `сохранить`; a color function `f(x, y)` — x, y are arrays of canvas coordinates, the response is (…, 3) sRGB 0–255. `область = (x0, y0, x1, y1)` of the canvas, without it — the whole. The gauges and `разность` by default take the tone of the paint without the light of the relief (`свет=False`), `взгляд` and `борозды` — with the light.

This is a variety of the trace, not of decisions: the lines are different even for identically given sweeps; the monotony of decisions is visible from the slant, the brush and the places (`.регулярность`) — they are measured by the sweep. |
| `х.регулярность(объект, этап, область, мазки, слой, журнал=None)` | the places of the strokes of a zone — a grid with jitter or uniform chance: `'сетка'`, `'случай'`, `'итог'` 0–1 (from 0.5 — looks like it), `'слово'`; from what: `'сосед σ/ср'` (the spread of distances to the nearest neighbor: grid 0.1–0.3, chance ~0.5), `'R'` (Clark–Evans: grid 1.4–2, chance ~1, clustered < 1), `'порядок'` (the vectors to the neighbor in a row, a square or a honeycomb — 1), `'шаг/сосед'` (the spacing to the next stroke by the journal against the distance to the neighbor: chance — leaps), `'шаг повторён'` (the share of spacings that repeat the previous one with an accuracy of 25 %: rows), `'ровность x'`, `'ровность y'`, `'ровность наклона'`, `'ровность длины'` (1 — the values are even over the range, as with uniform chance). ### Stroke response

`о = х.мазок(…)` — a tuple `(x0, y0, x1, y1)` of the written rectangle with a measurement of the trace: what came out of this touch, without a separate look. The canvas, brush, seeds and journal do not change from the measurement. `print(о)` — as a line: `(24, 14, 378, 87) · цвет L 74 C 0.025 h 262 #434b58 · закрыто 0.95, просветы 0.00 · ширина 39 px · край жёсткий (кайма 1.0 px, рваность 0.0 px)`. For a stroke without a trace the response is `None` (`ПустойМазок`), there is no measurement. `х.проба` gives the same response, as the first of its pair.

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| `о.цвет` | the tone of the trace on the canvas, as for `х.средний`: `{'L', 'C', 'h', 'rgb', 'hex'}` — the mean of the canvas tone (without the light of the relief, through the layers above) over the painted pixels of the trace: where the coverage of the new paint is not lower than half of `о.тело` (more than 16 thousand pixels — by every k-th). The shift from the given one — `разность_цветов(о.цвет['rgb'], цвет)`. If the trace is not visible — `None` |
| `о.тело` | 0–1: the coverage of the body of the trace — the 90th percentile of the coverage of the new paint under the brush; an opaque sweep ~1, a rub 0.2–0.4 |
| `о.закрыто` | 0–1: how far the new paint covered what lay under the brush — the mean coverage under the brush (where the brush touched the canvas) |
| `о.просветы` | 0–1: the share of the place inside the trace (between its rims, from the first paint of the sweep to the last) where the paint did not lie — the coverage is lower than half of `о.тело`: furrows down to the ground, islands of a dry brush, a broken sweep |
| `о.ширина` | px: the width of the trace across the sweep from rim to rim, the median along the sweep |
| `о.сырое` | `{'по сырому', 'подобрано', 'к положенному'}`: the share of the place under the brush where wet paint lay; what share of it the brush took off; what was taken off relative to what this stroke laid (by the volume of paint). Over dry and over the ground — zeros. `мастихин` also has `'на нож'` — the share of the wet under the knife that was cut off onto the knife and left the canvas (pressure from 0.85) |
| `о.разрывы` | a list of the places where the sweep broke: `{'от': (x, y), 'до': (x, y), 'длина': px, 'место': (доля хода от, до), 'причина'}`; `'нажим'` — the brush lifted off the canvas between touches, `'краска'` — the brush went on, but the paint did not lie (it ran out, a dry breakaway). The entry and the lift of the brush at the beginning and the end of the sweep are not a break; paint that ran out before the sweep did is a break to its end. Those shorter than 0.2 of the brush width (and 3 px) are not counted |
| `о.чужое` | a sweep over wet foreign color — `{'подобрано', 'лежало', 'названо', 'ΔL', 'Δh', 'Δab'}`: picked up — what was taken off relative to what was laid (as in `о.сырое`); lay there — the tone of the wet under the brush before the sweep (of its own layer, with `мешать_ниже` — also of the layers under it), weighted by thickness; named — the color of the sweep; the shift — the tone of the trace from the named one (`'Δh'` — `None` if one of them has a saturation lower than 0.02). It is there when all of this holds together: picked up ≥ 0.1; what lies there differs from the named one by ΔL ≥ 20 or Δa, b ≥ 0.05; the trace drifted toward what lies there by ΔL ≥ 8 or Δa, b ≥ 0.02 (the projection onto the path to it); the wet is on no less than a tenth of the place under the brush. Otherwise `None` |
| `о.серый` | the soft brush with opaque paint without thinner over dry where there is no paint in its layer yet — `{'закрыто', 'путь', 'лежало', 'названо'}`: path — what share of the path from what lies there to the named one the tone of the trace has passed (OKLab). It is there when path < 0.5, and the named one is far from what lies there (the same ΔL 20 or Δa, b 0.05). Otherwise `None`; a wiped one (`нагрузка=0`) in a new layer — `ПустойМазок` |
| `о.видно` | the share of the trace visible on the sheet, — not covered by the paint of the layers above (0–1): the product of `1 − покрытие` of the layers above the sweep, the mean over the trace, as of the moment of the sweep (what lay on top later does not go in here). 1 — the sweep is in the top layer, there is nothing above the sweep or there is no trace. A glaze does not cover (it colors what lies there, but that is visible); a wet top covers just as a dry one does. `print(о)` says so when less than 0.9 is visible: «видно на листе 0.00 — закрыл слой «верх»». The engine itself does not change the order of the layers. The measure is coverage, not light: under a dark glaze the sweep is almost not visible, while `о.видно` is 1 (a light sweep under two dark films: L of the place 0.3 → 0.8) — look at the result with `х.взгляд(область)` |
| `о.закрыл` | the name of the layer that covered the sweep most of all (if less than 0.9 is visible), otherwise `None` |
| `о.осталось` | live paint («Paint on the brush»): how much of it is left in the brush hair after the sweep, a share of a full brush 0–1; `None` — the paint is not live (the brush is full on every sweep) |
| `о.край` | `{'кайма', 'левый', 'правый', 'рваность', 'слово'}`: edge band — px from 10 to 50 % of `о.тело` across the sweep (the median; `левый`, `правый` — the sides relative to the direction of the sweep); raggedness — px, how much the rim wanders along the sweep; word — `'рваный'` (raggedness from 5 % of the brush width and 1.5 px), `'мягкий'` (edge band from 5 % of the width and 2 px), otherwise `'жёсткий'`. The edge measures the outer rim of the trace; the dryness of the sweep is in `о.просветы` and `о.закрыто`: a dry flat brush 40 px — gaps 0.18–0.41, covered 0.37–0.45 at any pressure, while the edge is from the pressure: 0.4 — «рваный», 0.6 — «мягкий», 0.8 — «жёсткий». If the trace is not visible — `None` |

`цвет`, `тело`, `закрыто`, `сырое`, `чужое`, `серый` are computed together with the stroke: +0.3–0.6 ms per stroke with a brush 6–12 px, +2–6 ms per sweep with a brush 48–140 px — 3–7 % of the time of the stroke; a sheet of 506 sweeps — 3 % longer; `чужое` and `серый` — another 2.5–10 ms (1–4 % of the time of the stroke). `ширина`, `просветы`, `разрывы`, `край` — on request: on the first access to any of them (or `print(о)`) the response unrolls the trace along the sweep from its own fields (0.7–2.3 ms) — they do not depend on what lay on the canvas later; while the response is kept untouched, it holds these fields (2 bytes per pixel of the rectangle), do not accumulate the responses of thousands of strokes as a list. On cores the numbers are the same as on one. If the responses are not needed at all — `Холст(…, замер=False)` or `х.замер = False` for the time of a batch: the response is only the rectangle; a replay of the journal does not compute the measurement. `видно` and `закрыл` are also computed together with the stroke; with the hints turned on the unrolling of the trace also goes together with the stroke — for the stage summary («Other public»), `print(о)` and the fields on request — as above.

Example on a clean canvas: an opaque sweep, a dry brush, a sweep with paint for three widths, a light sweep across wet, a sweep with pressure that fell to zero in the middle.


Thresholds of the encounter are from the test bench: flat, round, soft and splayed brushes 40 px, load 0.05–0.6, pressure 0.35 and 0.65, 832 sweeps over a wet fill. ### Gaps and scan

`п = х.просвет(область=None, слой=None, порог=0.5, площадь=4, дыр=50, путь=None, масштаб=1, без=None, до=None)` — a map of show-through: what share of the ground is visible through the paint in each pixel of the region. `слой='луг'` — through this layer and all those above it: what is under this layer is visible (`'видно': 'под «луг»'`). The computation is the same as for the picture: the light of what is below reaches the eye in the share (1 − coverage) · transmittance of each layer (for non-opaque paint the transmittance is the luminance of its transmittance), so that a veil and a glaze give a share, and not «yes/no». The canvas, brushes and journal do not change; `без`, `до` — as for `взгляд`.

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| `п.карта` | float32 (H, W) of the region: 1 — there is no paint, what is below is visible; 0 — covered |
| `п.дыры` | a list in descending order of area (the first `дыр`; `дыр=None` — all): `{'рамка': (x0, y0, x1, y1), 'центр': (x, y), 'площадь': px, 'просвет', 'у края'}` — a connected (8 neighbors) place where the map is not lower than `порог`, from `площадь` px; `'просвет'` — the mean of the map in the hole; `'у края'` — the hole touches the edge of the region: often this is not a hole but a place beyond the edge of the zone of strokes |
| `п.сводка` (`п['доля']`) | `'просвет'` — the mean of the map, `'доля'` — the share of the place from `порог` up, `'дыр'` — how many in all, `'в дырах'` — px, `'сквозь'` — the layers, `'видно'` — `'грунт'` or `'под «слой»'`, `'порог'`, `'площадь'`, `'область'` |
| `п.лист` | uint8 «canvas \| map» at `масштаб`, the frames of the 20 largest holes in red on both halves; `путь` — a PNG |

A whole canvas 2480×2380 — 2.3 s; a crop 160×200 — hundredths of a second. The map measures paint, not tone: a hole in a layer under which there is a layer of the same tone is almost not visible to the eye, but the map will show it — look at them side by side on `п.лист`.

`п = х.перебор(мазки, область=None, цель=None, путь=None, масштаб=1, ядра=None, мера=None, **параметр)` — a batch of strokes at several values of one parameter (or of several at once: lists of the same length, variant k takes the k-th value of each): `х.перебор(мазки, нагрузка=[0.006, 0.012, 0.025])`. Each value is a variant of `х.варианты` in its own process, from the canvas as it was at the call; the canvas, brushes and journal are not touched. A stroke of the batch is a dictionary of arguments of `х.мазок` (`{'точки': …, 'цвет': …, 'кисть': …, 'слой': …}`) or `(точки, цвет, кисть)` / `(точки, цвет, кисть, {доводы})`. A parameter is an argument of `х.мазок` (`нагрузка`, `цвет` — also a `Краска` with a kind, `касание`, `запас`, `ровно`, `нагрузка_вдоль`, `кисть`…) or a field of the brush (`ширина`, `сухость`, `кайма`…: the stroke goes with a copy of its own brush with this field; the points are the same). `точки` are not scanned — different places are laid by `х.варианты` with a piece. The seeds of the strokes are the same in all variants: the variants differ only by the parameter. `область` — without it, the frame of all strokes of all values (`прямоугольник_мазка`). If values gave the same picture — the warning `перебор: значения … ничего не меняет`.

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| `п[k]`, `len(п)` | `Вариант`, as for `х.варианты`; the chosen one — `х.взять(п[k])`: into the canvas and the journal, as if the strokes with value k had been painted directly |
| `п.значения` | `[{'нагрузка': 0.006}, …]` |
| `п.замеры` | for each variant, the tone without the light of the relief: `'средний'` — `х.средний` of the region; `'след'` — where L changed by 0.5 or more: `'доля'` of the region, `'dL'` (the mean), `'dL p5'`, `'dL p95'`, `'L'`; `'просвет'` — `х.просвет` through the lowest layer of the batch over the trace: `'просвет'`, `'доля'`, `'дыр'` (not at the edge), `'дыры'` [(center, area)], `'видно'`; `'мазки'` — the response of each stroke (`'L'`, `'тело'`, `'закрыто'`, `'просветы'`; an empty one or `замер=False` — None); `'однообразие'` — the score of the mirror; with `цель` — `'разность'` (`'среднее'`, `'σ'`, `'dab'`); with `мера` — `'своё'`: what `мера(участок, область)` returned (numbers, dictionaries, arrays) |
| `п.лист` | uint8 «before \| variant 0 \| …» three in a row, the label — the value, dL of the trace, show-through; `путь` — a PNG |
| `print(п)` | a line per variant: the values, L, the trace, dL, show-through, holes (with `цель` — dL to the target) |

Six values of load with a soft brush 72 px on a canvas 1000×300 — 0.85 s; on 2480×2380 each call costs, over and above the strokes, ~0.4 s (as `варианты`).

Example on a clean canvas: a dark layer, a dry sweep on top — show-through and holes; a veil with a soft brush at three loads — a scan, take the middle load.


### Coverage calibration

`н = нагрузка_для(кисть, покрытие=None, нажим=0.6, вид=None, разбавитель=None, толщина=None, px_на_мм=None, цвет=None, длина=None)` — the load as a number at which a single sweep with this brush over dry ground covers by `покрытие` (1 − show-through of the ground, as with `х.просвет`) or lays a film of `толщина` (h, as `Слой.толщина`), — by a table, without trials, in milliseconds. `по_таблице(кисть, нагрузка, нажим=0.6, вид=None, разбавитель=None, px_на_мм=None, цвет=None)` — the reverse: what a load will give. The brush is from a preset (`Кисть.из_файла`), the width in mm or px (px — with the `px_на_мм` of the canvas, without it 8). The kind is `'кроющая'` (without the argument), `'полукроющая'`, `'прозрачная'`; `'лессировочная'` (and turbidity 0 as a number) — only for `толщина=` (by the row `'прозрачная'`: thickness does not depend on turbidity), for `покрытие=` — `ValueError`; the thinner is 0 (thick, `None`) … 1, any in between (the nodes of the table are 0, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1). `цвет` is the paint of the sweep: sRGB (r, g, b) 0–255, a `Краска` (the kind is taken from it) or the answer of `цвет(L, C, h)`. **The coverage of non-opaque paint depends on color** — of transparent, semi-opaque and any with thinner greater than 0: light goes through the film, a light one lets more through than a dark one and at the same load covers 2–5 times weaker (flat 100 px, transparent, load 0.15: dark blue (70, 80, 110) — 0.50, yellow (200, 190, 60) — 0.22, almost white (240, 235, 225) — 0.09). For such paint `цвет` is obligatory: without it — `ValueError` «нужен цвет=…: покрытие не кроющей зависит от цвета». What does not depend on color is the coverage of opaque thick paint (it does not need `цвет`), the thickness and the share of any paint (`толщина=` — without color). The canvas, the brush and the journal are not touched. `длина` (mm of the sweep) — for a brush with live paint, «Paint on the brush».

| Field of `по_таблице` | What it means |
|---|---|
| `'покрытие'` | 0–1: 1 − show-through of the ground in the band of the measurement — the middle half of a sweep of 45 mm along its length, 80 % of the width of the trace (`о.ширина`) across; for non-opaque paint — with the light that passed through it, as with `х.просвет`: for the paint `цвет` |
| `'толщина'` | h: the mean thickness of the film in the band (`Слой.толщина`) |
| `'доля'` | 0–1: the share of the band where coverage is not below half of the body (the 90th percentile) — 1 − gaps, as with `о.просветы` |
| `'разброс'`, `'разброс_толщины'` | σ of a single sweep over the seed: one sweep is the number of the table ± that much. For a brush from 2.5 mm up this is 1–3 % of coverage; for a brush narrower than 2.5 mm (narrow bristle, liner) — 20–35 %: `плоская_узкая` 1.5 mm at 0.3 — 0.41 ± 0.08, `лайнер` 0.4 mm — 0.77 ± 0.12. For one narrow sweep the table is no authority, a series of sweeps converges to its number |

The table is `кисть/калибровка.json` (thinner 0 and 1) and `калибровка_р0.3.json`, `_р0.5`, `_р0.7` (without them a thinner between 0 and 1 is `ValueError`): preset × width × load × pressure × kind × thinner → the fields above; a node is the mean of eight sweeps (for a brush narrower than 2.5 mm — of thirty-two) with paint (70, 80, 110) over dry ground (166, 146, 129) on a clean canvas at 8 px/mm, the path is `путь_с_нажимом` with an entry and an exit of half a width. The axes: load 0.002–0.9 (15 nodes; the small loads of the soft brush 0.002–0.05 are among them), pressure 0.3 / 0.45 / 0.6 / 0.8, three kinds, thinner 0 / 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7 / 1; widths, mm: `плоская` 2–18, `мягкая` 1–15, `плоская_узкая` 0.75–2.5, `круглая` 1–7, `сухая` 2–12, `лайнер` 0.25–0.5, `мастихин` 2–8. Between the nodes — interpolation over log of load, log of width, pressure and thinner (by a straight line). For a brush with a point (`мягкая`, `круглая`, `плоская_узкая`) from 12 to 16 px (1.5–2 mm) the tip passes from pointed to laid flat, and coverage there rises in a twofold step (0.3 → 0.7 at load 0.2): the nodes are every 1 px. Accuracy (20 points between the nodes, `тесты/test_калибровка_5е.py`): the mean of the sweeps agrees with the table within 5.4 % and better; a single sweep with a brush from 2.5 mm up — within 8 %, for a narrow bristle and a liner the sweep itself wanders over the seed by 20–35 % (`'разброс'`): one sweep — up to 27 % from the table, a series — to its number. There is no color axis in the table: the laying of the sweep does not depend on color, therefore a node holds the show-through of the same sweep for ten gray paints from white to black, and the coverage of the paint `цвет` is computed over its channels by the formula of `х.просвет`; with liquid paint (with thinner) a light paint also darkens — the tones of the strands come together by pigment mixing — this was taken with sweeps of gray paints. With color (20 points × dark blue, yellow, almost white, `тесты/test_калибровка_5е2.py`): the mean of the sweeps is within 6.6 % of the table. Thinner 0.4 and 0.6 (16 points between the nodes × two paints, `тесты/test_разбавитель_Долгт.py`): the coverage of live sweeps is within 0.04 of the table. Below 0.3 there are no nodes except 0: any thinner greater than 0 turns on fluidity, and with `лайнер` the opaque at once becomes sparser (by 0.1 of coverage) — there the table overstates by up to 0.08, for the others — within 0.03. `мягкая` about 4.5 mm at pressure 0.4: the table understates coverage by 0.04–0.05 at any thinner.

Outside the table (width, pressure, load, thinner outside 0–1, a brush not from a preset) and the unattainable — `ValueError` in words with the nearest attainable, and not a silent limit: `нагрузка_для(мягкая 40 px, покрытие=0.95, нажим=0.45, разбавитель=1, цвет=(70, 80, 110))` — «недостижимо … наибольшее 0.845 при нагрузке 0.9; больше — шире кисть, плотнее (кроющая, без разбавителя) или темнее краска, или второй ход по высохшему». With light transparent paint one sweep cannot get much: flat 100 px with almost white (240, 235, 225) covers by no more than 0.42 — coverage 0.5 is unattainable for it, this is an error with that number. A brush field not as in the preset (`разброс_тона=0.3`) — a warning with the name of the field: the sweep may lie differently.

The table is by a single sweep over dry; for a series of overlapping rows — `план_покрытия` (below). A probe (`х.проба`, `х.перебор`) is needed where those are absent: for scattered sweeps over wet, over a layer lying there, with your own path (`жест` with a long entry, `нажим_вдоль`), with `ровно`, `по_гребням`, `запас`, `'сухая'`, with a set of paints, on your own ground or grain (`Холст(…, зерно=)`). Thinner cuts the coverage of a narrow brush in half, of a wide one — almost not at all: load 0.6, dark over light, 6 seeds, `о.закрыто` — `мягкая` 7 px 0.36–0.46 → 0.14–0.21 at thinner 0.5 (0.08–0.12 at 1), `круглая` and `плоская_узкая` 6 px 0.37–0.52 → 0.16–0.29, `плоская` 40 0.89–0.95 → 0.91–0.92 (0.59–0.62 at 1). A sweep shorter than 45 mm with a narrow brush covers more densely than the middle of a long one: paint decreases toward the middle of the sweep (reserve).

**Series of rows**. `п = план_покрытия(кисть, покрытие, шаг_рядов=0.8, сушка=False, нажим=0.6, вид=None, разбавитель=None, цвет=None, px_на_мм=None, проходов=None, длина=None)` — how many passes of parallel rows and what load per sweep will give `покрытие` of the middle of the field (1 − `х.просвет`, without the outermost rows); only computing, it lays nothing. Overlapping rows cover more densely than one sweep: at spacing 0.5 two sweeps lie on a point, and the load of `нагрузка_для` gives the series a tone much denser than ordered (flat 40 px for 0.6: that load — 0.116, a series with it at spacing 0.5 — 0.81; the plan — 0.060, the series — 0.605). `шаг_рядов` is the distance between the axes of the rows in shares of the brush width (`кисть.ширина`), 0.4–1.2; a pass is all the rows of the field once, the next pass is the same rows anew; `сушка=False` — passes one after another over wet, `True` — `х.подсушить(1.0)` between the passes; `проходов` — how many (None — the smallest at which the coverage is attainable, up to 8). The brush, kind, thinner, color — as with `нагрузка_для` (non-opaque paint needs `цвет=`).

| Key of the response | What it means |
|---|---|
| `'проходов'` | the number of passes |
| `'нагрузка'` | the load of each sweep of the series |
| `'покрытие'` | a list: the coverage of the middle of the field after each pass |
| `'разброс'` | σ of the coverage of the field over the seed (an estimate from σ of a single sweep) |
| `'шаг_px'` | the spacing of the rows on the canvas, px — the distance between the axes |
| `'одним_ходом'` | the coverage of one sweep with this load (`по_таблице`) — for comparison |

The law: show-through at a point is the product of the show-throughs of the sweeps that lay on it (this is how `х.просвет` computes), sweeps of different seeds are independent; therefore the depth of the series −ln of show-through is the sum of the cross profiles of depth of the rows, shifted by the spacing. The profile of a sweep across and three corrections are the table `кисть/ряды.json` and `ряды_р0.3.json`, `_р0.5`, `_р0.7` (preset × width × load × pressure × kind × thinner): a row over a wet neighbor of its own pass takes off part of its paint (transparent thick paint — up to half of the depth under itself) or, with a small load, flows into its gaps; the next pass over dried lies thicker (opaque with a small load — up to 1.2 times: it catches the relief), over wet — for transparent thick paint, less thickly (0.6–0.8: it takes off the former pass). A single sweep (`одним_ходом`) is from the table of `нагрузка_для`, its numbers are untouched. Check (`тесты/test_ряды_В3.py`: 16 points — flat 19 and 40, soft 32, round 22 × spacing 0.5 / 0.8 / 1 × wet and drying × opaque and transparent dark blue, orders 0.3 / 0.6 / 0.9): the mean of three live series is within ± 0.06 of the order (the worst — 0.04 out of 52 series); passes 2–3 one after another over wet (4 points) — likewise. The unattainable (more than what 8 passes at load 0.9 give; less than what one pass at 0.002 gives), spacing outside 0.4–1.2 and outside the table — `ValueError` in words with the nearest. Sweeps not in straight rows, scattered ones, ones over a layer lying there, ones in rows with different load — by a probe. A scattered series covers by the same law: a point under n sweeps of coverage c — 1 − (1 − c)ⁿ, that is, denser than a single sweep by 1.5–2 times already at two or three sweeps per point; measure it with a piece — `х.варианты` or `х.проба(…, область=)` and `х.просвет`.

```python
from кисть import Холст, Кисть, путь_с_нажимом, план_покрытия, нагрузка_для
к = Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=40)
п = план_покрытия(к, 0.6, шаг_рядов=0.5)
print(п, нагрузка_для(к, покрытие=0.6))              # {'проходов': 1, 'нагрузка': 0.05983, 'покрытие': [0.6], 'разброс': 0.017, 'шаг_px': 20.0, 'одним_ходом': 0.3931} 0.11611
х = Холст(600, 400, грунт=(166, 146, 129), px_на_мм=8.0)
ys = [80 + i * п['шаг_px'] for i in range(13)]       # поле рядами: оси через шаг_px
for проход in range(п['проходов']):
    for i, y in enumerate(ys):
        с = 100 * проход + i + 1
        х.мазок(путь_с_нажимом([(120, y), (480, y)], 40, нажим=0.6, вход=20, сход=20, сид=с), (70, 80, 110), к, сид=с, нагрузка=п['нагрузка'])
print(1 - х.просвет((210, int(ys[1]), 390, int(ys[-2]))).сводка['просвет'])   # 0.605 — поле кроет на 0,6; с нагрузкой 0.11611 одиночного хода — 0.807
```

**Furrows by number**. `б = борозды_для(кисть, σ=None, нагрузка=None, нажим=0.6, вид=None, разбавитель=None, ровно=None, сила=None, цвет=None, свет=True, px_на_мм=None)` — what `х.борозды` of the body of one sweep (the middle half of the length, the middle half of the width of the trace) will show, before laying; only computing, it lays nothing. With `нагрузка=` — a forecast; with `σ=` — an order: the load (and, if it cannot attain this, `ровно` or thinner) that gives this σ with the light (`свет=False` — without the light), and the forecast at this point. `ровно` and `разбавитель` that are given are not touched; of several loads — the largest (it covers more densely); a load below 0.2 (a rub, the ground through it) the order takes only if there is no other way. `разброс_тона` is from the brush (0–1); the color is the paint of the sweep, obligatory: σ follows the lightness of the paint (on the ground of the test bench a dark one, L 30, ripples almost twice as strongly as a middle one, L 90; with the light, at a large load the difference is smaller: flat 40 px, load 0.3 — 1.9 times, 0.9 — 1.3; with transparent paint — 1.1–1.8 times); saturation hardly counts (with transparent paint a saturated one is 10–15 % calmer than a gray one of the same lightness). `сила` is the strength of the light, 0–0.8 (default — the working 0.4). The brushes and widths of the table, mm: `плоская` 2–18, `мягкая` 1–15, `круглая` 1–7, `плоская_узкая` 0.75–2.5; narrower or wider — `ValueError` with the nearest (`плоская` 12 px at 8 px/mm is 1.5 mm, outside the table; a narrow sweep — `плоская_узкая` or a probe).

|---|---|
| `'σ'`, `'размах'` | σ and range of `х.борозды` with light of strength `сила` — the mean over the seed |
| `'σ_без_света'`, `'размах_без_света'` | the same with `свет=False` |
| `'разброс'`, `'разброс_без_света'` | σ over the seed: one sweep wanders by that much (for a narrow bristle laying thickly — 25–40 % of σ, for a wide one — 5–10 %) |

It is not the load alone that gives furrows: flat 40 px, load 0.6, dark blue — σ with the light 8.6 L; the same with `ровно=0.5` — 4.3; dark (30, 30, 40) — 12.7. Thinner r lays the sweep with `ровно` not less than r. The table `кисть/фактура.json`: preset × width × load 0.03–0.9 × pressure 0.3–0.8 × kind × (thinner 0 / 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7 / 1, `ровно`) × lightness of the paint L 30–245, a sweep of 45 mm on clean ground (166, 146, 129) at 8 px/mm; on your own ground, over paint lying there, in overlapping rows, with a short sweep — a probe and `х.борозды`. **Mixing over wet by number**. `п = смешение_для(кисть, лежит, цвет=None, доля=None, нагрузка=None, нажим=None, вид=None, разбавитель=None, ровно=None, толщина=None, подмалёвок=None, длина=None, холст=None, px_на_мм=None, слой=None, мешать_ниже=False, лежит_в=None)` — what one sweep over the wet paint lying there will come out like, before laying: how much of the tone of what lies there the body of the trace will take and what color it is. There is no need to dry everything for the sake of predictability: mixing over wet is computed in advance. `лежит` is a place of the canvas `(x0, y0, x1, y1)` with `холст=х` (the color — `х.средний` of the place without the light, the wet thickness — from the layers of the place) or the color of the wet paint with a thickness: `толщина=` (wet, units of `Слой.толщина`; thickness × moisture × (1 − 0.85 × degree of set), the sum of the layers) or `подмалёвок=` (the load of the fill 0.1–1); the wet thickness of the test bench is 0.07–2.1 (an ordinary underpainting with flat 40 at load 1 without `ровно` — 1.05–1.1). `цвет` is the paint of the sweep, obligatory (`Краска` — the kind is from it too). `длина` is the length of the sweep, mm (default 45, 10–200): over wet the brush gets dirty along the sweep, and the body of a long sweep is closer to what lies there — for a sweep of 105 mm it is 6–35 L darker than for 45 mm; give your own. With `нагрузка=` — a forecast; with `доля=` — the load to order (pressure is given — at it; not given — first 0.6, then 0.5, 0.65, 0.35, 0.8), of several loads — the largest; the unattainable — `ValueError` with what the sweep takes, the nearest and what to shift it with. The model's canvas, the brush and the journal do not change; the brush — any, as it is.

**The layer of the sweep** — the same keys as with `х.мазок`, and with the same meaning: `слой=` — where the sweep will lie (default `'основа'`, as with a stroke), `мешать_ниже=True` — to mix also with the wet of the layers under it. A sweep takes only the wet of its own layer, with `мешать_ниже` — of the lower ones too; the forecast counts the same: with `холст=` the thickness of what lies there is the wet of those layers of the place with which the sweep will mix, and the test bench lays it out by layers likewise (`п['слои']['раскладка']`): `'свой'` — the sweep into the layer of what lies there, `'ниже'` — into the layer above it with `мешать_ниже`, `'поверх'` — the wet is only under the layer of the sweep, and there is no `мешать_ниже`: there will be no mixing, the sweep will lie on top; the forecast gives the color of the trace then too (for transparent paint — with the show-through), but with `п['слово']` and a warning. Give the forecast the same `слой=` and `мешать_ниже=` as the stroke: the underpainting into `'подмалёвок'`, a sweep over it without `слой=` — into `'основа'`, and the difference from a sweep into the layer of the underpainting is up to 55 L. Without a canvas what lies there is in the layer of the sweep; in another one — `лежит_в='низ'` (a layer under the layer of the sweep). Paint in a layer above the layer of the sweep — also in words: on the canvas the trace will be under it. **Wet in two layers** (in the layer of the sweep and under it, a sweep with `мешать_ниже`) — the layout `'два'`: the test bench lays the lower one with its own color and wet thickness, above it, wet — the paint of the layer of the sweep, the sweep — into it (with a single fill of the color of the place the forecast was darker than the live one by 29–54 L); over even wet — within 2 L, over furrows (σ/mean of the thickness of the layer > 0.2) — `п['слово']` «прогноз мимо до 10 L», more accurately — `х.проба`; three wet layers and more — the lower ones as a single fill, in words.

| Key of the response | What it means |
|---|---|
| `'доля'` | the share of the tone of what lies there in the body of the trace: where its color lay on the segment «paint of the sweep → what lies there» in OKLab; 0 — pure paint, 1 — the tone of what lies there; for transparent paint the show-through of what lies there through the film enters too, more than 1 — the film darkens what lies there; slightly below 0 (−0.07) — the trace went farther from what lies there than the paint of the sweep, the color (`'L'`, `'rgb'`) is correct. This is the share of the **tone**, and not `о.сырое['к положенному']` (the share of the volume taken off): they diverge (forecast 0.39 with «к положенному» 0.16–0.28) — compare your own sweep with `'ответ'` |
| `'L'`, `'C'`, `'h'`, `'rgb'`, `'hex'` | the color of the body of the trace (the middle half of the length of the sweep, the middle half of the width, without the light) — as `х.средний` of this place |
| `'разброс_L'`, `'разброс_доли'` | over the seed: one sweep wanders by that much (a bristle laying thickly, in the transition «covers — mixes», — 5–15 L; opaque paint laid thickly and transparent paint — 1–3) |
| `'ответ'` | the mean `о.цвет` and `о.сырое` of the sweep — compare with the response of your own stroke |

| `'нагрузка'`, `'нажим'` | what to lay with (with an order — the fitted ones) |
| `'слои'` | `{'ход', 'мешать_ниже', 'мешается', 'не_мешается', 'выше', 'раскладка'}` — the layer of the sweep, the layers of the place with which the sweep will mix and will not, the layers with paint above it, the layout of the test bench (`'свой'`, `'ниже'`, `'поверх'`, `'два'`) |
| `'слово'` | only when the forecast is not about mixing (`'поверх'`) or paint lies above the layer of the sweep, and with `мешать_ниже` and wet paint in the layer of the sweep and under it — also when the wet is in furrows, there are three wet layers or more, or in the layer of the sweep or under it there is less of it than the fill of the test bench lays — the same in the words of the warning |

How it computes: there is no table, it is the sweep itself on its own test bench in memory (`кисть/смешение.py`) — the color of a trace over wet does not follow a single number: one share of the mixture per node, taken with one pair of paints, is wrong by up to 30 L on another pair. The test bench: smooth ground (166, 146, 129), the underpainting — a fill with one opaque paint by `плоская` 5 mm in rows, `ровно=1` (wet thickness up to 0.75; thicker — rows without `ровно` up to 1.05, thicker still — two passes up to 2.1), a sweep of 45 mm straight across the rows in a layer by the layout, 24 seeds; a forecast — 2–3 s, an order — 8–40 s. Orders 0.2 and 0.5 at the pressure of the point: 16 out of 32 are attainable, the live is within tolerance at 14; the rest — `ValueError` with what the sweep takes. Your own path, the grain of the ground, a transparent or many-layered underpainting, what lies there in patches — closer to `х.проба`. **Edge by number**. `п = край_для(кисть, кайма_мм=None, нагрузка=None, нажим=None, по_сырому=None, холст=None, место=None, слой=None, поле=True, длина=None, px_на_мм=None, разбавитель=None, цвет=None)` — what edge (`о.край`) one sweep will give, before laying, and what to take for an ordered edge band; only computing, it lays nothing. A soft edge is not searched for by trials and drying: it is ordered by a number. The edge band depends on the brush, the field `кайма`, the load, the pressure and the wet under the sweep, and over dry and over wet it goes in different directions: `мягкая` 32 over dry is wider with a light hand (load 0.05 and pressure 0.35 — 0.35 mm, load 0.8 — 0.12 mm at any pressure), over wet of thickness 0.39 — the reverse: load 0.05–0.1 — 0.06 mm, 0.2–0.3 — 0.33–0.44 mm. Wet less thick than 0.1 suppresses the edge band, 0.3–0.4 gives the widest one, 0.7 — narrower again. With `нагрузка=` (and `нажим=`, default 0.6) — a forecast; with `кайма_мм=` — an order: the load and the pressure (if pressure is given — only at it), and if the brush as it is does not suffice — the field `кайма` (0.15, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7; `поле=False` — do not touch it); lay with `п['кисть']` (a copy of the brush with this field) with `нагрузка=п['нагрузка']` and the pressure `п['нажим']`. The wet under the sweep: `по_сырому=None` — dry ground; `True` — the fill of the test bench (wet thickness ~0.39); a number — the wet thickness of the layer of the sweep (units of `Слой.толщина`, as with `смешение_для`, the test bench 0.10–0.72); or `холст=х, место=(x0, y0, x1, y1)` — the wet of the layer of the sweep `слой=` (default `'основа'`) in the place; the canvas is only read. A sweep takes only the wet of its own layer: the wet in another layer does not change the edge band. Wider or narrower than the attainable — `ValueError` with the nearest, a checked sweep, and with what to make it by instead: wider than 0.7 mm is not possible with one sweep (an order also passes within the tolerance max(0.08 mm, 25 %): `мягкая` 48 over dry gives 0.72 for an order of 0.9) — by blending on top (gauge — `х.переход`, the recipe «Patch with a falloff»).

| Key of the response | What it means |
|---|---|
| `'кайма_мм'`, `'кайма_px'` | `о.край['кайма']` — the mean over the seeds, mm and px |
| `'разброс_мм'`, `'шум'` | one sweep wanders by that much (σ over the seed); from 0.08 mm — 48 seeds and `'шум'` in words: the mean of the sweeps hits the order, one sweep — within ±2σ (over wet σ 0.1–0.2 mm) |
| `'слово'`, `'слова'`, `'рваность_px'` | `о.край['слово']` of the majority of the seeds and the count of the words; raggedness — as in `о.край` |
| `'нагрузка'`, `'нажим'`, `'поле'`, `'кайма_кисти'`, `'кисть'` | what to lay with: with an order — the fitted ones; `'поле'` — `{}` or `{'кайма': v}`, `'кисть'` — a copy of the brush with it (only with an order) |
| `'сырое'` | `{'толщина', 'откуда'}` — the wet under the sweep by which it was computed |
| `'цена'` | in words, what was paid for the edge: «кайма 0,45 мм — ценой: закрыто 0,27, ход бледнее заказанного цвета на 86 L: след L 128, краска L 42» or «— без цены: …» (covered from 0.85 and the tone within 10 L of the paint, counting only the shift toward what lies there: the other way — without a price at any shift) |
| `'закрыто'`, `'тон'`, `'бледнее'` | the numbers of the price: `о.закрыто`, L of the body of the trace, by how many L it went from the paint toward what lies there. The paint is `цвет=` (without it — that of the test bench (40, 40, 60)), what lies there is the ground of the test bench or its light fill: on your own sheet the shift of tone is its own |
| `'сидов'` | how many seeds are in the count: 24, at a spread from 0.08 mm — 48 |

What one sweep can get (the test bench, 8 px/mm; check — `тесты/test_край_К5.py`, 12 points against the mean of 12 live seeds): `мягкая` 32 and 48, `плоская` 40 — 0.12–0.5 mm both over dry and over wet (wider — with the field `кайма`: `плоская` 40 over dry up to 0.62 mm, `мягкая` 48 up to 0.71–0.81); `круглая` 22 — over dry up to 0.34 mm, over wet up to 0.23 mm, 0.5 mm — `ValueError`. How it computes: there is no table — the model's brush is any, with any fields, and the edge band does not follow a single number; it is the sweep itself on its own test bench in memory (`кисть/край.py`): smooth ground (166, 146, 129), a straight sweep of 50 mm (`длина=` 10–200) with dark paint into `'основа'`, over wet — a fill by `плоская` 5 mm in rows along the sweep, `ровно=1`, with light paint; a forecast — 24 seeds, 2–7 s; an order — a scan of load 0.05–0.85 × pressure 0.35–0.85 with 6 seeds each and a check of the best with all the seeds: over dry 20–110 s, over wet 70–150 s. Your own path, the grain of the ground, the wet in furrows or patches — closer to `х.проба`.

**Lightness limit**. `д = предел_светлоты(кисть, цвет, подложка, нагрузка=None, нажим=0.6, проходов=1, сырая=False, L=None, свет=False, px_на_мм=None, предел=False)` — what L the middle of a field of dense rows (spacing ¼ of the width, gesture `'подъём'`) will come out at with this paint over the underlayer and where its ceiling is. The computing is a live test bench: the field is laid on its own canvas **with this very brush** (with a copy, as it is: any width, its own `разброс_тона`, a brush not from a preset), with this paint (a `Краска` with a kind — too) and over the same kind of underlayer 16 times, each field has its own sweeps and its own underlayer; the canvas, the brush and the journal of the model are not touched. `цвет`, `подложка` — sRGB or lightness L as a number. `проходов` 1–8: over dry each one over the dried former one; `сырая=True` — a sweep into the same wet layer of the underlayer (it will mix with it), the passes one after another. **Three calls:** a forecast (with `нагрузка=` or with the load of the brush) — L at this point; `предел=True` — the ceiling as well (lighter with light paint, darker with dark paint did not come out with any load 0.1–0.9 and any number of passes 1–3: a scan with three fields per point, the point found anew with 16 fields — it is the one in the response; the call's own point, if it came out farther, replaces it, beyond this grid too (load 1, 8 passes — 250.5); the best is picked by three fields, and a neighbor may be no worse: in the example below one pass with load 0.9 — 250.25, the limit — 250.0 ±0.2); `L=` — an order: the load (0.1 · 0.15 · 0.2 · 0.28 · 0.4 · 0.6 · 0.9) and the passes that give it most cheaply (picked by three fields, the two cheapest anew: a cheaper point may be missed — `плоская` 6 mm with white paint over L 60, order 244.6: the response 0.6, 0.4 is enough), or `ValueError` with the nearest attainable and with what else will help. The same arguments — the same response. **Why the white paint is not white:** the paint on the brush is undermixed (`разброс_тона`), the tone of a strand is a multiplier to the color, and there are no strands lighter than white, therefore the dark strands darken, and the light ones are clipped. White paint (255, 255, 251) with `мягкая` 6 mm over dried creamy (250, 238, 205) — 249.3 at load 0.5, the limit 250.0; three passes are no lighter than one. The coverage meanwhile is 0.99: the underlayer has nothing to do with it. Only more even paint raises it higher: `кисть.с(разброс_тона=0.1)` — 253.7, 0 — 254.4. Dark paint runs into the reverse: through the gaps the light underlayer is visible (black `плоская` 6 mm over creamy at 0.35 — L 27.0 with the paint 20).

| Key of the response | What it means |
|---|---|
| `'L'`, `'сидов'` | the mean of 16 fields of the test bench: L of the middle of the field (`х.средний`, without the light; `свет=True` — with the light of the relief) |
| `'σ_поля'`, `'ошибка_прогноза'`, `'σ'` | by how much one field wanders over the seed (soft 0.2–1, flat and round 1–4, over wet 0.5–7, palette knife with a small load 10–20: it lays in patches); by how much the mean itself is not known (σ_field / 4); both together — your own field will lie within `L ± 2σ` in 9 cases out of 10 |
| `'предел'`, `'предел_нагрузка'`, `'предел_проходов'`, `'предел_ошибка'` | only with `предел=True`: the ceiling (with dark paint — the floor), what it is attained with and the error of its mean |
| `'по_проходам'`, `'нагрузка'`, `'проходов'` | L after 1…`проходов` passes; the point of the response (with an order `L=` — the one found) |
| `'причина'` | by how many L they lead away from the paint: over dry `'тон прядей'` and `'подложка'` (gaps), over wet `'смешение с сырой подложкой'`; with `свет=True` — also `'свет рельефа'` |
| `'краска_L'`, `'подложка_L'`, `'тон'`, `'покрытие'`, `'толщина'` | L of the paint and of the underlayer; L of the layer of the sweep without the underlayer, its coverage and thickness (over wet — `None`: there is one layer) |
| `'слова'` | the same as a string |

The test bench (`кисть/предел.py`; in mm — the same at any `px_на_мм`, without it 8): ground (200, 190, 170), the underlayer — a layer by `плоская` 12.5 mm with load 0.6 with its color, the rows of the field — no fewer than 7 sweeps of 40 mm each (for a brush wider than 6 mm — 6.67 widths), the window of the measurement — the middle of the field. There is no table: there is no grid of axes, an edit of the physics of the sweep does not make the forecast outdated. **Time** (a measurement, 8 px/mm, up to 10 processes; the underlayers are kept in the memory of the process, and a second call over the same underlayer is faster): a forecast with one pass — 4–7 s with a brush up to 6 mm, 16 s with a brush of 10 mm; two or three passes with a brush of 10 mm and wider — 20–37 s; `предел=True` — 10–50 s; an order `L=` — 9–43 s; the same arguments repeated — at once. **Accuracy** (40 new cases, all the presets, 28 over dry and 12 over wet, a live field by someone else's code and seeds): the forecast against the mean of 16 live fields — within 2 errors for 37 out of 40, farther than 2.3 not one, there is no bias either over dry or over wet; one live field within `L ± 2σ` — 93 %. The test bench does not compute (`ValueError`): load 0 and above 1, pressure outside 0.05–1, more than 8 passes, a canvas of the field larger than 6 Mpx (a wide brush at a large `px_на_мм`). The field of the test bench is straight rows over an even underlayer on its ground: scattered sweeps, your own ground with grain, thinner — by a probe.

### Manner from a reference

`м = х.манера(область=None, образец=None, область_образца=None, px_на_мм_образца=None, объект=None, этап=None, слой=None, журнал=None, свет=True, окно=8.0, путь=None, без=None, до=None)` — a gauge of the manner of a canvas region by the picture and, with `образец`, a gauge of the reference with the same ruler and the difference. Everything is measured by the picture, for the canvas too — with the same computation; the strokes — by parsing the picture into candidate strokes (`'мазки разбором'`). Both pictures are first brought to 4 px/mm by their own scale, therefore their millimeters are the same: `px_на_мм_образца` — how many px per mm the painting of the reference has (without it, for a picture — as for the canvas, and the report says so; for a `Холст` and an `.npz` — their own). `образец` — a path to a PNG/JPG, an sRGB 0–255 array, a `Холст`, a path to an `.npz` or a ready `Мерка`; `область_образца` — a piece of the reference in its px. `свет=True` — the canvas with the light of the relief, as in an image of a painting. The strokes of the canvas — by the journal of the canvas (or `журнал`): selection `объект`, `этап`, `слой`, without them — with the center in the region. The canvas, the brushes and the journal do not change.

A gauge without a receiving canvas: `from кисть.манера import мерка`; `мерка(картинка, px_на_мм=None, окно=8.0, что='картинка', область=None, свет=True, предел=None, сторона=None, файлом=True)` — the same `Мерка` (the picture — as `образец`, but without `px_на_мм` — 8 px/mm, and the words of the gauge say so; `предел` mm — strokes by parsing no smaller than this: to compare two images of different resolution — with `м['предел']` of the coarse one; `сторона=` — px of the long side of the whole painting, when the picture is a cutout of it: the shares of the stroke are counted from it; with `область` and for a canvas it is known); it can be given to `х.манера(образец=…)` and `х.однообразие(образец=…)`. **Time (Mn-4):** a picture given by path (PNG, JPG) is measured once: the gauge is written next to it as the file `<картинка>.мерка-<ключ>.npz`, and the same call — and `х.манера(образец='путь', …)` — reads it in fractions of a second; if the size in mm, the region, the limit, the picture itself or the code of the gauge is another — it is measured anew. An array and a canvas are measured every time. The first time: a crop 600×600 px — 5 s per core, a painting 1600 px — 15–25 s (the time goes by the mm of the painting: the computation is at 4 px/mm; under someone else's load longer). If the folder is read-only — without a file; `файлом=False` — neither write nor read.

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| `м.холст`, `м.образец` | a `Мерка`: `.сводка` by the sections below (`мх['края']`), `.слова`, `.массивы` (maps 4 px/mm (except: `'жёсткие'`, `'мягкие'` — 2 px/mm; `'направление'`, `'порядок'`, `'длина'`, `'ширина'` — by nodes every half of `окно`, `'шаг'` — in px of 4 px/mm): `'картинка'`, `'направление'`, `'порядок'`, `'жёсткие'`, `'мягкие'`, `'тон в пятне'`, `'длина'`, `'ширина'`; `'хорды'` — a list (width mm, x0, y0, x1, y1 in px of 4 px/mm)), `.px_на_мм`, `.размер` (mm) |

| `'тон в пятне'`, `'цвет в пятне'` | σ of lightness L (and the length of the deviation a, b OKLab) around what is blurred by a patch of 4 mm, per pixel: p10, p50, p90, mean |

| `'штрих'` | `'длина'`, `'ширина'` — over how many mm the correlation of the texture (lightness minus that blurred over a patch) along the direction of the place and across it falls to half, shifts 0.5–16 mm (shorter — 0.5, longer — 16): p10, p50, p90, mean, σ/mean, `'эфф'` of octaves, `'на полу'` — the share of places shorter than the first shift of 0.5 mm (for an image with a limit coarser than 0.5 mm — shorter than the limit, it is in `'пол'`; if there are more than half of such places — the numbers are None, and in the words `'пол'` instead of 0.5 mm). This is a streak of the texture inside a patch (brush hair, a drag), not a whole stroke. Where the canvas or the reference has more than half of the places on the floor, the length or the width of the streak does not go into the difference, and in the words — «уже 0,5 мм в N % мест — не различается» |
| `'ширина мазка'` | the width of a patch-sweep across the direction of the place, mm, scale 1–20, correct at ~2–15 mm (a sweep narrower than ~2 mm reads as 2 — «does not distinguish», but the number stays; without numbers — only by `'предел'`): along lines every 2 mm across the sweep — chords between edges (a drop of lightness and color, blurred by 0.5 mm, over ±1 mm from 8 L, as for `'края'`). An edge with one mix on both sides of it (the means closer than a palette step of 0.02 in a, b and 16 in L) does not break a chord: bands of the charge and furrows of the brush hair inside a sweep are not edges, and overlapping sweeps of one mix are measured as one patch, as the eye sees them. A chord is a stroke, if its edges run as a ribbon along the sweep (on a neighboring line the same edges ±0.5 mm) and there is texture inside: an even ground or a fill (texture finer than 0.5 mm in the middle of the chord is more even than 1 L) is background, not a stroke, and a gap of ground of 2–3 mm between sweeps is background too; a chord counts, if the place at its middle has a sweep of this direction (sectors of 30°) and a coherence from 0.15. p10, p50, p90, mean, σ/mean, `'эфф'` of octaves, `'хорд'`, `'шире'` — the share of chords wider than 20 mm, `'фон'` — the share of the area without texture (finer than 1 mm more even than 1 L), `'пол'` — 2 mm or the limit, whichever is larger, `'на полу'` — the share of chords no wider than the floor (with a margin of 0.25 mm), `'шкала'` — (1, 20). This is a patch or an object between edges, and not strokes one by one — the strokes are in `'мазки разбором'`. If there are fewer than 20 chords (`МАЛО_ХОРД`; a small cutout of the reference: across 50–60 mm 5–6 strokes of 10 mm lie) — «мало, в разницу не идёт». Wet-on-wet, where neighboring strokes are closer than 16 L and the color step, the edges between them are one mix, there are few chords and the gauge is silent; the width of your own strokes — by the journal (`м.холст['мазки']['ширина']`, in the words «мазки журнала … ширина») |
| `м.холст['мазки']` | by the journal: `'мазков'`, `'длина'`, `'ширина'` — mm (mean, σ, σ/mean, p10/p50/p90, eff), `'наклон'` — as for `однообразие` |
| `м.разница` | {feature: {`'холст'`, `'образец'`, `'во сколько'` = reference / canvas}}: `'замесов'`, `'тон в пятне'`, `'цвет в пятне'`, `'края'`, `'жёстких'`, `'наклон'`, `'у соседей'`, `'порядок'`, `'длина штриха'`, `'разброс длины'`, `'варианты длины'`, `'ширина штриха'`, `'разброс ширины'`, `'ширина мазка'`, `'разброс ширины мазка'`, `'мазков на дм²'`, `'длина мазка разбором'`, `'ширина мазка разбором'`, `'длина к ширине'`, `'протяжек'`, `'без мазков'`, `'прямых краёв'`, `'переход края'`, `'сход мазка'`, `'тупость схода'` (these ten — at a common resolution in mm: a sharper picture is measured scaled down to the limit of the coarse one, therefore its numbers here are not the same as in its own gauge), `'длина мазка в долях'`, `'ширина мазка в долях'` (shares of the side, at a common limit in shares: a painting of 1 m and a canvas of 25 cm are compared by them, and not by mm); for a streak on the floor — `'во сколько'` None and `'на полу'`, for a stroke width from a small number of chords — None and `'хорд'` |
| `м.места` | with a reference — by cells of 50 mm (if the reference is smaller than a cell — a cell by its smaller side): the cells of the canvas where the spread of tone in a patch (p50 of the cell) or of mixes (90 % of the area of the cell) is 1.5 times or more smaller or larger than in the reference (the median of its cells of the same size); dictionaries `'рамка'` (px of the canvas), `'тон в пятне'`, `'замесов'` — (canvas, reference), `'во сколько'` — reference / canvas for each, `'слово'` — `'однообразнее'`, `'пестрее'` or both; the worst first; a cell at the edge smaller than half does not go in; without a reference — [] |

| `м.лист` | uint8 «picture \| directions \| edges and chords of the stroke width (yellow — narrow, magenta — wide) \| strokes by parsing (outlines)» — the row of the canvas, the row of the reference; `путь` — PNG |

The spread of color in a piece of oil and in a smooth sketch (`мерка(…, свет=False)`, «цвет в пятне» p50 — the length of the deviation a, b OKLab): a Levitan sky 0.0046, a Constable sky 0.0034, Korovin 0.0033, Monet 0.0103; a smooth sketch — 0.0006 (the sky, the top 700 px) and 0.0005 (the whole). In oil it is 5–8 times wider (in Monet — 17). «Тон в пятне» p50: oil 3.1–3.5 L (Monet 8.5), the sketch 0.4 L. The palette (`'замесов'`) and σ a, b over the whole piece are of the same order for a sky in oil and a sky of the sketch (9–18 and 7 mixes): the difference is between neighboring places, and not over the whole sky.

A region of 2480×1600 of the canvas at 8 px/mm with a reference of 523×432 — 4 s. The numbers are a ruler for comparing the canvas with the reference by one computation, and not a measure of the painting: an image of a reference carries grain, varnish and compression, the canvas does not have them — look at `м.лист` alongside. In an image with blurred edges and without a common sweep there are few ribbons (`'хорд'`): the stroke width there is shaky.

An example on a clean canvas: a row of identical sweeps against a reference canvas where the sweeps are of different length, slant and width; then the stroke width — rows of sweeps of 3 mm against rows of 1.25–10 mm, without overlap.

## Stroke journal

JSONL: one line — one JSON; empty lines and lines with `#` are skipped. Coordinates and sizes — journal px; `--scale k` multiplies them all. The journal — a record of painting, which Python carries out; a replay — for rebuilding a sheet and for editing someone else's journal.

### The journal writes itself

`Холст(…, журнал='лист.jsonl')` — each `мазок`, `снять`, `прижать`, `подсушить`, `схватить`, `размочить`, `к.вытереть()`, `к.набрать(…)` writes a line by itself, in the form in which the replay reads it (the lines of the brush — before its next stroke; for a brush without live paint — only what is on it by that stroke); the file is written anew. `python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --out лист.png` gives the same sheet.

- **Rollback.** `с = х.снимок()` … `х.откатить(с)` — the journal is trimmed to the place of the snapshot, byte for byte; `х.проба(…)` does not go into the journal.
- **Labels and notes.** `х.пометка('край', этап=4, объект='край', намерение='…')` — the line `{"mark": …}`; the stage and the object go as the fields `"stage"`, `"object"` into all the lines after it (except the lines of the brush: `"brush"` without a stroke, `"load_brush"`, `{"wipe": …}`, `{"rinse": …}`). `х.мазок(…, намерение='мягкий край')` — `"intent"` (`объект=`, `этап=` — the stroke's own). `х.заметка({'отвергнуто': …, 'замер': {…}})` — `{"note": …}`: what you saw, what you rejected, the measurements before and after. The replay does not execute labels and notes.
- **Selection.** `python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --list --stage 4 --object край` — the lines of the stage and the object with numbers; with `--out` instead of `--list` — a picture of only these strokes (drying and brushes — all). From Python — `from кисть.повтор import отобрать; отобрать(open('лист.jsonl'), этап=4, объект='край')`.
- **Continuation.** `х.сохранить('этап4.npz')` writes the line `{"saved": "этап4.npz"}`; `Холст.загрузить('этап4.npz', журнал='лист.jsonl')` trims the journal after it and writes further; the cut tail — in `лист.jsonl.до_отката` (`.2`, …) and as a line in stderr («State» above).
- **Subareas.** The strokes of `х.участок(…)` in this process are written into the journal of the canvas; a subarea painted in another process does not carry a journal — `вписать` will warn.
- **Typos.** In a journal with `"physics"` an unknown field — an error with the line number and the nearest name: `журнал, строка 12: нет поля lvel, может быть level?`; in a journal without `"physics"` (recorded outside a canvas) — a warning, the field is skipped. An unknown key of the brush — the same (but it is an error without `"physics"` too, and the words also carry the name of the brush): `нет поля разброс_тонна, может быть разброс_тона?`.

| `"pressure_along"` | none | pressure along the sweep `[[доля 0–1, множитель ≥ 0], …]`; 0 — the brush is above the canvas, the sweep breaks into pieces. With the field the flat laid flat touches with a width according to pressure: weak pressure — with the middle, by 0.55 — with the full width |
| `"rotation_along"` | none | the rotation of the brush in the fingers along the sweep, `[[доля, градусы от хода], …]`: 0 — across the sweep, 90 — edge-on; the width of the trace ≈ `W·|cos φ| + 2` px, edge-on (from ~80°) — a band as thick as the tuft, like `"touch": 0` (a brush of 5 mm — 1.25–1.5 mm; for a brush without thickness, `пучок_мм` 0, — 2 px). With any of the three fields along the sweep the rotation is held by the hand |
| `"tilt_along"` | none | the tilt of the handle, degrees 0–85: the strands stretch farther, by a factor of 1 + 2·sin(tilt) |
| `"azimuth_along"` | 0 — back along the sweep | where the handle is tilted, degrees from the sweep: the strands stretch that way |
| `"angle"` | random | a jab only: the direction of the brush, degrees from the x axis clockwise; the imprint of the flat — across |
| `"touch"` | 1 | flat: 1 — laid flat, 0 — on its edge (a band as thick as the tuft, the brush field `пучок_мм`) |
| `"stripe"` | one band per paint | a set laid thickly: the width of a band on the brush hair, px; the paints alternate in little bands |
| `"stripe_len"` | over the whole sweep | the length of a piece of paint in a band, px: pieces with a ragged edge, between them the neighboring paint or a slit; under a light hand longer |
| `"layer"` | `"основа"` | layer name |
| `"mix_below"` | false | to mix also with the wet paint of the layers below |
| `"ridges"` | none | 0–1: with a light hand (pressure ≤ 0.3, fades out by 0.6) — pieces on the tops of the ridges of the paint lying there, the furrows are empty; `0.5` — the pieces are wider; over an even place — a film. Not with `"сухая"` |
| `"level"` | none | 0–1, opaque: the film closes into an even layer without furrows, the tone is the named one. In detail — below |
| `"medium"` | none | 0–1: thinner — the film is thinner and more transparent, it flows for non-opaque paint too; neither with `"сухая"` nor with `0` |
| `"reserve"` | none | >0: dry breakaway — paint for this many widths; neither with `"сухая"` nor with `0`, nor for the palette knife |
| `"fibers"` | none | 0–1, only with `"load": "сухая"`: the body is dense, the edge — fibers of 2–3 px along the sweep; with another load and for the palette knife — an error |
| `"load_along"` | none | soft only: release of paint along the sweep `[[доля, 0–1], …]`, the shares in ascending order. In detail — below |
| `"tool"` | none | `"тряпка"` or `"скребок"`: to remove the wet, not to lay paint; `"черенок"` — to scratch out: the wet is pushed apart into beads; `"нож"` — to cut off the dried with a sweep of the knife (`х.прижать`). In detail — below |
| `"depth"` | none | only with `"tool": "нож"`: mm, the blade does not sink during a sweep deeper than this many below the highest point under the knife; does not change with `--scale` |
| `"lines"` | none | written by the engine itself for a sweep with a brush of tufts (the brush field `просветы`): the lines of the tufts `[[длина касания px, ширина пучка px, нажим пучка сверх его порога касания], …]` — they are read by the mirror `х.однообразие`; the replay lays the sweep by the other fields, it does not read this one; do not write it by your own hand |
| `"wipe"` | false | to wipe the brush before the stroke |
| `"dry"`, `"set"` | none | to dry the whole canvas or let it set before the stroke |
| `"opacity"` | 1 | mixing in as a film |
| `"brush_tone"` | 0 | 1 — a portion of paint wanders in tone as a whole |
| `"seed"` | with `"seeds": "tree"` in the header — from the sheet, the stage, the object and the points of the line; without — from `--seed` and the line number | the seed of the stroke; the canvas always writes it |
| `"path_seed"`, `"path_rng"` | `"seed"` | for a line with `"ctrl"` — the seed of the path (`жест(…, сид=)`, when it is not the one of the stroke) or the state of the generator PCG64 before the call `жест(…, rng)`: `[state, inc, has_uint32, uinteger]`; the canvas writes it |

A brush in the journal is one per pair «preset, size»: the dirt goes from stroke to stroke, as long as there is no `"wipe": true`. A brush with `"brush"` — each name has its own, like an object in Python.

### Fields in detail

**`"points"`.** The trace goes along the polyline itself (a corner stays a corner), the pressure and the angles between the points — in a straight line; there is no entry, exit or default pressure: a soft landing — a first point with pressure 0–0.2. The columns: `x, y` px; `нажим` 0–1; `dt` — ms per step (none or `null` — 1 ms per px; more — the hand is slower); `наклон` of the handle 0–85°; `азимут` — degrees from the sweep (0 — the strands back along the sweep); `поворот` — degrees on the canvas from the x axis clockwise: the wide side of the flat stands across it; between the points — by the shortest arc; without the column the flat turns after the sweep by itself. A point repeated on the spot with `dt` — the brush stands still. With `"ctrl"`, `"end"`, `"entry"`, `"pressure"`, `"angle"` in the same line — an error (`"path_seed"`, `"path_rng"` — too); `"pressure_along"` multiplies the pressure of the points; the `*_along` fields of the angles replace their own column.

**`"level"`.** The sweep displaces the wet paint of its own layer lying there: a second overlapping sweep — flush with the first, there is no seam. Sweeps with `"level"` of one layer that lay end to end or overlapping over wet close along the whole length of the seam; where they diverge as a wedge — the flooding ends in a concave arc. If the spacing of the rows is wider than the brush by 3 px or more — a gap remains. It does not close with a sweep without `"level"`, of another layer, with a dried one. A set `"полосы"` or `"ряд"` remains a set: the film is even, the border of the paints is 1–3 px; `"смесь"` and `"кончик"` come together into one paint. For non-opaque paint, `"сухая"` and `0` it has no effect.

**`"load_along"`.** The hand eases the brush off: the width and the edge band are the same, the film is thinner by that factor — the tone fades along the sweep without a change of pressure. An eased-off brush does not spend paint and does not touch the wet paint lying there: a sweep can be started with 0 above the wet. With the field the film lies along the path of the hair — on an arc the inner side is no thicker than the outer one; for sweeps in a circle always set it, even if `[[0, 1], [1, 1]]`. For bristle and with a lift-off of the brush (`"pressure_along"` with 0) — an error. In Python — `нагрузка_вдоль=`.

**`"tool"`.** The path — `"ctrl"` or `"points"` (Python writes `"core"`), the pressure — `"pressure"`, `"end"`, `"pressure_along"`; `"layer"` — to remove only from this layer. `"нож"` (`х.прижать`) — a blade edge of `"size"` px laid flat: it cuts off the dried and the wet above the plane of the blade, the depth is led by the pressure and `"depth"`; Python writes it with `"core"` points. The rag — a wad of cloth of `"size"` px: a pressed one (from 0.6) touches with the whole wad; a light hand of 0.2–0.35 removes a third to a half of the film in bands of the cloth, a pressed one of 0.5–0.8 — two thirds or more, a thin tone remains; toward the end of a long sweep it removes more weakly. The scraper — a hard edge of `"size"` across the sweep: under it the wet is removed down to the ground, what was removed, as a bank, flows past the ends of the edge as a bead; on partly dried paint (moisture 0.4) there is no bead — a lump at the lift. The handle end — a hard round end with a diameter of `"size"`: a furrow with beads at the sides, the volume of paint is the same; pressure from 0.8 — down to the dry layer or the ground, weaker — a film under the end; `"seed"` has no effect (for a `"ctrl"` path — the seed of the path). What is dry and what has set is not touched. The other stroke fields have no effect (except `"dry"`, `"set"` — before the removal, and for a `"ctrl"` path — `"entry"`, `"path_seed"`, `"path_rng"`; `"brush"` — an error); a malformed name — an error.

### Running a replay

```bash
python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --out лист.png --no-light --layers слои
```

Options: `--out` (required, except with `--list`), `--list` (print the lines, do not lay), `--cores N` (how many processes compute the strokes, the result is bit for bit as on one; «Speed and memory»), `--stage`, `--object` (only the strokes of the stage and the object), `--width`, `--height`, `--ground R G B`, `--scale k` (multiplies the coordinates, the width and the hair spacing of the brush and `px_per_mm`; a journal without `px_per_mm` at scale k — a canvas of 8·k px/mm), `--preset имя` (for all strokes without their own), `--seed`, `--no-light`, `--light сила` (0.40), `--shadow [сила]` (without a number — 0.6), `--save лист.npz`, `--from лист.npz`, `--start N`, `--layers папка` (RGBA layers, thickness, light), `--resume`, `--checkpoints папка`, `--checkpoints-max ГБ`, `--no-checkpoints`, `--every N`, `--every-sec С` (checkpoints — below). The journal `-` — from standard input. From Python: `from кисть.повтор import повторить; х, n = повторить(строки, масштаб=1.0)`.

**A draft lies about the soft and the fine**: at a scale < 1 the summary of the replay names how many strokes of a soft and of a narrow brush of this run lay below the tuning of the physics (what holds px — «Scale» in «Concepts»): `черновик ×0.25 (2 px/мм, физика настроена на 8): ниже настройки легли 412 мазков мягкой и 1300 узкой кисти (уже 24 px здесь) — воздух и тонкое судить по 1:1`; a soft narrow one counts as soft; with `--scale 1` there is no such line.

**Checkpoints and `--resume`.** The replay itself writes the state (the same as `--save`) into the folder `<имя --out>.точки/` (another one — `--checkpoints папка`): before each label of a new stage, once per 5 minutes of computation (`--every-sec С`, 0 — not by time), every N strokes (`--every N`) and after the last line (except after the last line — only if at least one stroke has been laid since the previous checkpoint of this go). A checkpoint of a sheet of 2480×2380 — about 200 MB and 10 s of writing. Once per 30 s it prints the progress to stderr: `строка 4012 из 19543 (21 %), мазков 2003 из 9777, этап 3; прошло 2:10, осталось ~7:50`. `--resume` starts from the nearest checkpoint up to which the journal has not changed: after a break-off — from the last one, after an edit of a line — from the last checkpoint before it (an edit in stage 5 does not lay stages 1–4 anew), with lines appended at the end — from the end of the former journal. The result — the same `.npz` bit for bit as a replay from the beginning. Checkpoints from which the journal has diverged (an edit above them) go away from the folder; a checkpoint written with another `--scale`, `--seed`, `--preset`, `--width`, `--height`, `--ground`, `--stage`, `--object` or with other physics is not taken and goes away. Without `--resume` the replay goes from the beginning; valid checkpoints remain and are not written anew.

What is kept in the folder: stage checkpoints — the last checkpoint of each stage (for past ones this is the checkpoint before the label of the next one, for the current one — the freshest) — and up to 12 intermediate ones as a ladder: denser nearer to the end of the journal, sparser farther away. The intermediate ones are the other checkpoints: by `--every`, by time and the end of each go «appended — `--resume`»; therefore an edit of a recent stroke inside a long stage starts from a checkpoint not far before it, and not from the beginning of the stage. The excess goes away by itself, the intermediate ones first: the one whose departure will on average add the fewest strokes to the recomputing (edits of the recent weigh more: the weight of an edit d strokes from the end ~ 1/(d+1)); stage checkpoints — when no intermediate ones remain, the one without which the neighboring ones stand closest (the last one remains). A sheet of 2480×2380 (6 stages, 9761 strokes, a checkpoint ~220 MB, writing ~11 s), 30 goes «appended — `--resume`» over the whole sheet, a limit of 2 GB: 9–11 checkpoints in the folder, 1.8–2 GB; an edit of a stroke 40 strokes before the end of the journal — from a checkpoint 80–271 strokes before the edit, 13–14 s; an edit near the end of the past stage, a thousand strokes from the end of the sheet, — from a checkpoint ~1500 strokes before the edit, ~100 s, and not from the beginning of the stage (~5000). The limit of the folder — `--checkpoints-max ГБ` (2; 0 — without a limit: there are no more than 12 intermediate ones then too); if one checkpoint is larger than the limit — there are no checkpoints, the replay goes without them. One long run without goes writes intermediate ones once per 5 minutes; more often — `--every N`. About each deletion — a line in stderr: `убрал точку лист.точки/строка_0019331.npz (строка 19331, конец журнала, 219.7 МБ): папка больше предела 2.00 ГБ; промежуточная: без неё соседние точки в 50 мазках друг от друга, следующая — в 0 мазках от последней`. Fragments of writing (the process was killed while it was writing) go away at the next launch (an `.npz` without `.json` — if it is older than 60 s, a file `….часть<PID>` — if there is no process with this number). `--save` with checkpoints — a hard link to the checkpoint of the end, and not a second file (on another disk — the file itself); writing over `--save` does not touch the checkpoint. Each `--out` (and `--checkpoints`) has its own folder: variants, scales, `--stage` — each has its own; one not needed — `rm -r лист.точки`.

`--resume` with `--from` — an error: `--from` takes a `--save` file, `--resume` — a folder of checkpoints; with `--from` there are no checkpoints (and `--checkpoints` — an error). `--no-checkpoints` — do not write them (with `--resume` — an error). A replay and a continuation after an edit:

```bash
python3 -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --out лист.png --every 2 && python3 -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --out лист.png --resume
```

From Python: `from кисть.повтор import собрать; х, n, с, начало = собрать(строки, 'лист.точки', продолжить=True, печать=print)` — the lines of the journal as a list, `начало` — from which line this go lay; the fields as for `повторить` (except `состояние`, `начало`, `точка`; `печать=None` — no progress, deletions of checkpoints — to stderr) and `каждые=`, `каждые_с=` (300), `раз_в_с=` (30: how often to print the progress), `предел=` (GB, 2; `None` — without a limit). `повторить` has `точка=` for the same: the call `точка(положена, строка, этап_и_объект, мазков)` before each line that the replay executes (before a label and a note too; while there is no canvas — not called). The checkpoints of a folder — `точки_папки('лист.точки')`: (line, path of the `.npz`, what is written).

**Painting on.** `--save лист.npz` writes everything on which the next stroke depends: the layers, the wetness, the brushes with dirt, the scale, the seed, the preset, the line number. `--from лист.npz` lays the lines after the saved one; `--start N` — from line N (counting from 1). Two goes — the same `.npz` byte for byte as the whole journal. For lines without `"seed"` in a journal without `"seeds": "tree"` the seed comes from the line number: do not touch the lines above the place of painting on, append at the end; an edit of a laid line — a rebuild from an earlier `.npz`. `--scale`, `--seed`, `--preset` with `--from` are taken from the file (another value given by the option — an error); `--width`, `--height`, `--ground` have no effect; the light is given each time. From Python: `с = загрузить_состояние('лист.npz'); х, n = повторить(строки, масштаб=с['масштаб'], сид=с['сид'], пресет=с['пресет'], состояние=с, начало=с['строка'] + 1); сохранить_состояние('лист.npz', с)`.

## Recipes

The numbers are working ones, not exact: check with a probe on your own canvas. A recipe — by a Python call; in parentheses — the journal field, if it is named differently. Notation: `к` — the brush, `W` — its width, `сид=…` — the seed of the path, each sweep has its own (a generator `rng` for the whole stage shifts the paths of the following ones), `опоры` — `[[x, y], …]`. A path with an end — `жест(опоры, W, конец, сид=…, нажим=…, вход=…)` (`"end"`, `"pressure"`, `"entry"`); pressure along the sweep — `х.мазок(нажим_вдоль(точки, профиль), …, нажим_руки=True)` (`"pressure_along"`); rotation of the brush along the sweep — `углы_вдоль(точки, поворот=профиль)` from `кисть.повтор` (`"rotation_along"`); a jab of the flat at the angle `у` — the points `[[x, y, 0.85, 30.0, 0.0, np.radians(у), у + 90.0]]` (`"angle"`).

| What is needed | How to lay |
|---|---|
| **Mass, a patch of one color** | `плоская`, sweeps overlapping by a third of the width, the ends inside the series by `жест(…, 'остановка')` or `'отрыв'`; `'подъём'` gives a fringe along the bottom of the series. A blunt start — `вход=3`–`5` px (the flat and the dry brush 48 px; from 8 the nose gets rounder); the default entry (0.5–1.7 of the width, not less than 6 px and not longer than 0.32 of the sweep; for a sweep shorter than 1.5 widths it is shorter) lands the brush as a little nose — the series comes out «as a row of pencils» |
| **Even fill** | one paint, `Кисть.из_файла(…, разброс_тона=0.3)`; the load for a coverage — `нагрузка_для(к, покрытие=0.97, нажим=…)` before laying (for a wide bristle of 100–140 px — 0.54–0.62 at pressure 0.6, at 0.45 — 0.53–0.59; at 0.8 — 0.62 for 100 px, for 140 px 0.97 is unreachable; the pressure is 0.3–0.8, otherwise an error; 0.24–0.30 covers by 0.83–0.88 and lets the ground through in the furrows — «Coverage calibration»), for a set — up to 0.35; overlapping rows over wet cover more densely than one sweep — by a probe |
| **Impasto strike in two or three paints** | the color as a set `[(rgb, доля), …]` (`"colors"`), `раскладка='полосы'`, `нагрузка='пастозная'`: the bands are sharp, the border is 0–2 px |
| **Even film in two or three paints in bands** | `плоская` 24–48 px, a set, `раскладка='полосы'`, `нагрузка=0.8`, `ровно=1` (`"level"`): without furrows, the border of the paints is 1–3 px; narrower bands — `полосы=(3, None)` (`"stripe"`); to merge — `раскладка='смесь'` |

| **Voluminous impasto sweep** | `плоская` from 16, `'пастозная'`, pressure from 0.7: ridges along the sides, with `'остановка'` — a bank at the end; a light hand, a load up to 0.35, `мягкая`, `ровно` give no ridge |

To order — `край_для(к, кайма_мм=…, по_сырому=True)` («Edge by number») |
| **Thin line** | `плоская_узкая` or `круглая` 3–12 px, load up to `'обычная'`, `жест(…, 'подъём', сид=…)`; with the flat on its edge — `касание=0`: a band as thick as the tuft at any pressure (a brush of 2.5 mm — 0.75 mm, 5 mm — 1.25–1.5 mm), finer or thicker — `к.с(пучок_мм=0.5)` … `2` |
| **Line and plane with one brush** | `плоская`: laid flat — a plane, the same sweep with `касание=0` — a line or a rim of 1–2 mm; in one sweep — `углы_вдоль(точки, поворот=[[0, 0], [0.25, 0], [0.7, 90]])`: laid flat and goes over onto its edge without steps (`примеры/ребро.py`) |
| **Short mark without a feather** | `жест(…, 'остановка', сид=…)` (the feather is given by the exit of `'подъём'`, not by the entry) |
| **Dot** | a jab — one point `[[x, y]]`, the narrow or the round, `'пастозная'`; for the flat — a jab at an angle (see above) |

## What the engine cannot do yet

- A stroke from your own points (not `жест`, `путь_с_нажимом`, `тычок`) or with corrected points the journal stores in full as core points (`"core"`): a strike of 400 px — ~30 KB as a line. A line `"points"` written by your own hand is replayed not bit for bit with the same call from Python — fractions of a level.
- The strokes of a subarea painted in another process do not get into the journal (attempts in processes — `варианты`). One journal is written by one Python process: pieces painted by several sessions are not brought together into one journal.
- Handle end: the end — a hard hemisphere, the handle stands upright — there is no tilt, no flat cut and no point; a clean band along the axis — 2–4 px for an end of 3 mm too; in the pits of the grain and of the furrows of a dry layer paint remains (an end of 3 mm over a grain of 0.5 mm opens only the tops of the threads); a bead that went past the edge of the canvas is lost; in a piece of `варианты` it does not work; the mirror and the hints do not count its sweeps.
- A brush of tufts (`веерная`, `растрёпанная`): there are no calibration tables for them (`нагрузка_для`, `по_таблице`, `план_покрытия`, `борозды_для` answer with `ValueError` «кисть не из пресетов»; the load — by a trial: `х.проба`, `х.перебор`; `к.хватит` does count); the coverage of a sweep — 0.45–0.55 of the trace for the fan one, 0.65–0.7 for the splayed one; a thin wet film does not close over the gap between the tufts (a liquid one — floods the whole trace); the lines in `"lines"` — where a tuft touches the canvas, and not where it had enough paint; for a fan the spacing of the lines is in px, not in mm: on a canvas of 16 px/mm the same brush in mm gives twice as many lines twice as fine (measured: 10 mm, pressure 0.5–0.9 — 1.4–1.6 times as many lines, in mm they are 1.4–1.6 times finer); the wide side of the fan follows the sweep with a lag, as with the flat one, the rotation in the fingers — `углы_вдоль`.
- `"fibers"` — only for `"сухая"`; for a brush of 24 px the slit at the edge is there, but not along the whole length of the sweep; `"ridges"` over even ground lays a film.
- Palette knife: it does not spend live paint (`к.набрать`) — for each sweep the knife takes the full `нагрузка`; it has no undermixing (`разброс_тона`); layers below its own are hard for the knife (`мешать_ниже` has no effect); a place that the sweep has passed over, the same sweep does not touch on a second turn; what was cut off onto the knife (pressure from 0.85) does not return to the canvas. The rag does not smear paint and does not give it back; the bands of the cloth are even, at the spacing of the thread; a light rag of 0.2 removes a third.
- Not all of the physics is in mm: a narrow brush (up to ~24 px at 8 px/mm), the rim of a brush laying thickly, growing together, the dry and the soft brush, numbers right in the code hold px of the canvas; for an impasto sweep with the light, at 4 and 16 px/mm the tone of the body differs from 8 px/mm by up to 1.6 L.
- Blending levels the relief of the paint lying there: in the middle of the falloff the brush hair of the patches fades to nothing, little furrows of soft hair remain — finer than bristle ones (the slope of the relief across the sweep is ~9 times smaller than for the brush hair of the flat one), and a coarse bumpiness of the patches of 10–20 px; the step at the joint itself — up to ~2 L.
- A flowing layer closes relief narrower than ~1 mm (the ripple of fine painting, the furrows of brush hair); a wave wider than 1.5–2 mm (the overlap of rows of a wide brush) it repeats. The closing does not touch wet paint lying there (over wet — `ровно`, blending); in drying the relief does not slump by itself. A sweep without fluidity does not level dried relief: over dry ripple a thick sweep lies as ripple.
- Thinner from 0.9 (and `ровно=1` with an opaque one) erases the trace of the brush: the body is smooth, the rim is a step of a pixel, at ×4 — a flat cutout. A film with the trace of brush hair — 0.5–0.7.

## Speed and memory

One core, numpy; canvas 2480×2380, one sweep over a clean canvas (median, measurement of 22.11): `плоская` 12 px, a sweep of 150 px — 0.01 s; 48 px, 300 px — 0.05 s; 100 px, 400 px — 0.1 s; 140 px, 500 px — 0.18 s; `мягкая` 48 px, 300 px — 0.04 s; 100 px, 600 px — 0.17 s. `х.картинка(свет=True)` of the whole sheet — 0.6 s; `проба` of a stroke — as the stroke itself. A replay of the journal of a sheet of 2480×2380 in 14 096 strokes (`--cores 1`): 1:1 — 360 s, the draft `--scale 0.5` — 137 s, `--scale 0.25` — 81 s; a sheet of 2480×2380 in 9 761 strokes at ¼ — 74 s. A draft is faster not by as many times as there are fewer pixels: for a small stroke the time goes to the hairs, and not to the pixels. An overlapping sweep over wet is more expensive than a sweep over dry (canvas 600×600, a sweep of 100×400 px, `'обычная'`, the second–fourth sweep, median, a step of sweeps of 20 px, a slow core of the PC; on a fast one — 0.15 / 0.17 / 0.08 / 0.025 s, and a sweep of `плоская` 100 over clean canvas — 0.08 s): `плоская` 100 — 0.28 s, `мягкая` 100 — 0.32 s, `плоская` 40 — 0.16 s, `круглая` 12 — 0.05 s (before the speed-up of 22.11 — 0.35 / 0.38 / 0.17 / 0.05; the bytes are the same); the L4 piece «sky», 500 strokes of `плоская_т03` 140 px (a preset of that sheet, it is not in `пресеты/`) on one core — 189 s (was 222). A chain of such sweeps is not divided among cores (the frames intersect) — its price is exactly the price of the underpainting and of an attempt at a piece in `х.варианты`.

The computing of a sweep holds in memory ~80 KB per point of the path for a brush of 48 px (a sweep of 26 thousand points — 2 GB; the raster of the trace goes in pieces and takes nothing beyond this). Lay a sweep longer than ~15 thousand points with a wide brush in overlapping pieces.

A sweep over one place is expensive: 18 spirals of the soft 48 px with a radius of up to 530 px (163 sweeps over dry with drying, the path of the brush is 2.9 million px, the brush passes over each place ~100 times) — 13.5 min and a peak of 2.7 GB; a sweep of 16 thousand points with a brush of 48 — 5.6 s, of them the raster of the trace 1.2 s, the transfer of paint by steps ~1.5 s, the rest — the computing of the hairs; there is no single bottleneck. A sheet of 2480×2380 in ~11 000 strokes: the brushes — ~10–15 min of computing. Strokes in different places of the canvas are computed on cores by themselves («Cores» below); several attempts at a piece at once — `варианты`.

**A large canvas** (test bench 6400×4000 at 8 px/mm with grain, 2 cores, `prlimit` 8 GB; 3000 sweeps in two layers — below are the numbers of «Scale»): strokes — ~16–19 per second, this is exactly the price of the sheet; a replay of the same journal with a checkpoint — 167 s. Output and saving are computed in bands in threads (by the canvas cores, no more than 4): `х.png` — 5–7 s (was 17–24), `х.картинка()` whole — 3 s and a peak of the main process of 2 GB (was 18 s and 4.9 GB: the light of the whole canvas in one piece holds ~180 bytes per px), `х.сохранить` — 5–7.5 s (was 9–14), `х.взгляд` 1200×800 and `х.виды` — 0.5 s, `х.однообразие` — 0.2 s, `х.варианты` 3 × 700×700 — 3.4 s. The peak of all processes — 4.1 GB (for `варианты` — up to 5.5 by the sum of RSS, shared pages are counted in each process). The height of the grain of the ground is computed once per canvas and is held in memory (100 MB at 6400×4000). The files `png` and `.npz` — the same bytes as before.

**Cores.** Strokes are computed on several processes by themselves — both in painting from Python (`х.мазок`) and in a replay of the journal. The number of processes: `Холст(…, ядра=N)`, `Холст.загрузить(…, ядра=N)`, `повторить(строки, ядра=N)`, `собрать(…, ядра=N)`, `python -m кисть.повтор лист.jsonl --out лист.png --cores 12`; without a number — `KIST_CORES` from the environment or the machine's cores, no more than 16 (a given number too: it takes no more than 16); `1` — everything in this same process, as formerly. In the calls and in the journal nothing changes. At once go the strokes whose rectangles (frames of strokes: the trace of the strands with margins) do not intersect; those that intersect go in the order of the calls; a brush not wiped between strokes waits for its previous stroke. The result — the same canvas (all the fields of the layers), the same brushes and the same journal byte for byte as on one core: a sheet of 2480×2380 in 10 939 strokes by replay and random sheets by painting and by replay have been checked.

Painting on cores: `х.мазок` accepts a stroke (seed, counter, journal line — immediately, in order) and returns without waiting for the pixels; the response — deferred (`Холст.мазок`, above). Everything that reads or edits the canvas other than by a stroke first finishes computing the strokes itself: `картинка`, `png`, `рельеф`, `проба`, `снимок` and `откатить`, `участок` and `вписать`, `подсушить`, `схватить`, `снять`, `сохранить`, `убрать_слой`, `порядок_слоёв`, `х.пустые`, reading of a layer array (`х.слои['проба'].толщина`) and of what is on the brush (`к.копия()`, `к.грязная()`); explicitly — `х.досчитать()`. What to expect: (1) a layer array taken into a variable before the strokes does not wait for what is being computed — take it anew or call `х.досчитать()`; (2) the word `ПустойМазок` and a stroke error (`ValueError` with the number of the stroke of the canvas) sound when the stroke has finished computing — on a later call; after an error the canvas is alive, but the strokes accepted after the erroneous one are already laid (on one core the script would have stopped at it); (3) while a snapshot is alive (`х.снимок()`), strokes go immediately, in this same process; (4) a child process (fork, a pool) computes by itself, on one core: its canvas layers are shared with the parent for reading, before the first write it takes a copy of them — its strokes are not visible to the parent; branch — after `х.досчитать()`; (5) a stroke that needs a new layer array (the first non-opaque paint of the layer, the first flowing sweep, the first stroke with thinner), a sweep with live paint, a stroke while a tape is on, a stroke on a subarea, and the first 48 strokes of the canvas go in this same process; a new layer — the processes are started anew; (6) `х.закрыть()` stops the processes (with the next stroke they will start again); without it they go away together with the canvas and the script. If a stroke were to name a frame wider than the one given to it by its own computing — the error `ТесныйПрямоугольник`, and not «almost the same»: compute with `ядра=1` and tell the Engine.

What this gives in time (12 cores against one, measurement of 17.11, a PC of 8 fast cores with two threads and 4 slow ones): a random sheet of 3000 strokes scattered over 2480×2380 — 7 s against 36 s (5 times); a sheet of 2480×2380 in 10 849 strokes, where strikes lying side by side go one after another, — 257 s against 413 s (1.6 times); the same sheet with 90 sweeps over one place (spirals of the soft one, ~80 % of the computing of the sheet) — 28.5 min against 34 min. Cores speed up only strokes in different places of the canvas: a stroke whose frame intersects the frame of an earlier one must go after it — this is exactly the order of painting over wet. On that sheet the main process is busy for 27 s out of 257 (the rest it waits for the processes), and the longest chain of such strokes — ~160 s of computing: its underpainting separately — 500 strikes with a brush of 140 px 420–560 px long in a grid of 240×60, each one overlapping its neighbors in the row and the rows above, — 240 s on one core, 165 s on 12 with a chain of 141 s. The sheet will not come out faster than the chain on any number of cores; it is shorter — when strikes neighboring in order lie in different places of the canvas. A sweep over one place goes into the queue and on cores is no faster. A stroke on cores is computed up to its frame twice (7–14 % of its time, the first time — in advance, in another process); a stroke before which there are no unfinished ones (a sweep over one place) — once. Memory: the layers — the same as on one core (shared memory of the processes, not copies); beyond that — the working arrays of one stroke for each process and the points of up to 512 strokes of the queue; the peak on that sheet — 9.3 GB on 12 cores and 9.9 GB on one (`time -v`; it is held by the long spirals).

**A layer in tiles.** A layer field (`цвет`, `покрытие`, `толщина`, `влажность`, `схват`, `пропуск`, `текучая`) lies in tiles of 64×64: a tile takes memory when a stroke writes into it something other than what was in it; an untouched one and an even one (all of it one number — so it is after drying and setting of the whole canvas) take no memory. A layer costs as much as is covered — to lay an object into its own layer is not expensive. Measurement (one process): a layer of 120 jabs on a canvas of 2480×2380 — 11 MB (as whole fields it would be 165); the state of a sheet of 2480×2380 (17 layers) after loading — 503 MB of process memory (as whole fields — 2845; the fields — 455 MB: two large layers 122 and 108, the others 7–27); `.npz` and all the fields — the same bytes as with whole fields, on 1 and on 4 cores; a look at 400×400 after a stroke — 0.01 s, the whole sheet — 1.9 s. How much is taken — `х.память()` (name of the layer → bytes). On cores the tiles — in the shared memory of the processes, likewise by what is touched.

A field is read as a numpy array, only a slice — a copy, and not a view:

```python
import numpy as np
from кисть import Холст, Кисть
х = Холст(400, 300, ядра=1)
х.мазок([[60, 80], [300, 120]], (200, 90, 40), Кисть.из_файла('пресеты/плоская.json', ширина=20.0), слой='низ', сид=3)
т = х.слои['низ'].толщина            # Плитки: (В, Ш) float32, как массив
кусок = т[60:140, 40:320]             # срез — копия (numpy-массив): менять его — поле не меняется
всё = np.asarray(т)                   # всё поле одним массивом (собирается по надобности)
т.max(), т.shape, (т > 0).sum()       # методы, арифметика и функции numpy — как у массива
т[0:10, 0:10] = 0.0                   # писать в поле — только присваиванием
х.память()                            # {'низ': байт, …}
```

`.npz` (`сохранить`) — the former one, the fields whole: old files are read, a loaded canvas is in tiles again. The rectangle of a stroke on cores is checked by the grid of tiles: strokes that go at once do not share tiles.
